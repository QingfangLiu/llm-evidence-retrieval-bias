I've excluded reviews, meta-analyses, protocols without outcome data, and guidelines.

**Randomized controlled trials (efficacy/effectiveness)**

1. Prochaska JJ, Hall SE, Delucchi K, Hall SM. "Efficacy of initiating tobacco dependence treatment in inpatient psychiatry: a randomized controlled trial." Am J Public Health. 2014;104(8):1557-65. N=224, acute locked psychiatry unit, San Francisco. Motivational treatment + NRT vs. usual care; verified abstinence significantly higher in intervention arm through 18 months. PMID 23948001.

2. Metse AP, Wye P, Elton-Marshall T, et al. "Efficacy of a universal smoking cessation intervention initiated in inpatient psychiatry and continued post-discharge: a randomised controlled trial." N=754 across four Australian psychiatric inpatient facilities. PMID 28195010.

3. Stockings EA, Bowman JA, Baker AL, et al. "Impact of a postdischarge smoking cessation intervention for smokers admitted to an inpatient psychiatric facility: a randomized controlled trial." N=205, one Australian facility. Nicotine Tob Res. 2014. PMID 24939916.

4. Hickman NJ 3rd, Delucchi KL, Prochaska JJ. "Treating Tobacco Dependence at the Intersection of Diversity, Poverty, and Mental Illness: A Randomized Feasibility and Replication Trial." Uninsured, ethnically diverse psychiatric inpatients with co-occurring substance use. PMID 26180227.

5. "Bridging the Hospital Discharge Gap: Pilot Trial of a Digital Smoking Cessation Program for Psychiatric Inpatients." Randomized pilot trial comparing a digital smoking cessation intervention adapted for psychiatric inpatients against brief counseling, assessed at 1 and 3 months postdischarge. Psychiatric Services, 2026.

6. Prochaska JJ, Gill P, Hall SM. "Treatment of tobacco use in an inpatient psychiatric setting." Psychiatr Serv. 2004;55(11):1265-70. (Early controlled trial/pilot preceding the 2014 AJPH study; cited as PMID reference in later trial reports.)

**Secondary/combined analyses of trial data (still primary empirical data, not reviews)**

7. Prochaska JJ, et al. "Treating Smoking in Adults with Co-Occurring Acute Psychiatric and Addictive Disorders." Combined data (n=216) from two inpatient-initiated RCTs, focused on the SUD subsample. PMC5537041.

8. Metse AP, Bowman J, et al. "Uptake of smoking cessation aids by smokers with a mental illness." Analysis of intervention uptake (N=378) from the Metse/Bowman RCT dataset. PMC5012253.

9. Schuck RK, Dahl A, Hall SM, et al. "Smokers with serious mental illness and requests for nicotine replacement therapy post-hospitalisation." Tob Control. 2016;25(1):27-32. Data from the large (~1,100-participant) Stanford inpatient trial (NCT00968513). PMID 25209524.
