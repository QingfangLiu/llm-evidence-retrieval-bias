**Trabecular micro-bypass stents (iStent / iStent inject)**
The iStent inject pivotal trial was a prospective, randomized, single-masked, multicenter trial in which eyes with mild-to-moderate POAG were randomized 3:1 to ab interno iStent inject implantation versus no stent, both after cataract surgery, followed for two years (Samuelson et al., Ophthalmology 2019). A related 5-year, prospective, randomized, multi-surgeon trial compared two trabecular bypass stents against prostaglandin monotherapy as stand-alone treatment for newly diagnosed, treatment-naive POAG (Ophthalmology Glaucoma).

**Schlemm's canal microstent (Hydrus)**
The HORIZON trial series is a prospective, multicenter, single-masked, randomized controlled trial comparing cataract surgery with Hydrus Microstent implantation versus cataract surgery alone, with published 2-year, 3-year, and 5-year outcome papers, plus a separate report of the US cohort results and a post-hoc visual-field progression analysis.

**Ab interno trabeculectomy (Trabectome)**
Multiple prospective cohort studies from the Trabectome Study Group, including outcomes after failed trabeculectomy, in exfoliation versus POAG, in African American versus Caucasian patients, and by baseline IOP tertile.

**Excisional goniotomy (Kahook Dual Blade)**
Prospective case series across multiple populations (North America, India), plus a key head-to-head RCT.

**Gonioscopy-assisted transluminal trabeculotomy (GATT)**
Multiple prospective/retrospective cohort studies across severity strata and populations, plus a registered RCT protocol comparing GATT to bent ab interno needle goniotomy.

**Ab interno canaloplasty ± trabeculotomy (OMNI system)**
The GEMINI study (prospective, multicenter, interventional, 12-month and 36-month extension) and the related ROMEO study.

**Head-to-head comparative trials**
Notably the Falkenberry RCT directly comparing KDB goniotomy to iStent, and a Japanese RCT comparing KDB goniotomy to microhook ab interno trabeculotomy on trabecular meshwork morphology and IOP.
