## Anti-TNF agents

**Infliximab** — ACCENT I randomized 573 CDAI ≥220 patients who had responded to a single infliximab infusion to placebo, scheduled 5 mg/kg, or 5 mg/kg→10 mg/kg maintenance every 8 weeks. Patients receiving scheduled maintenance infliximab were significantly more likely to sustain clinical remission than those on episodic/placebo maintenance (odds ratio 2.7, 95% CI 1.6–4.6), with similar rates of serious infection across groups.

**Infliximab vs. azathioprine vs. combination (SONIC)** — 508 biologic- and immunomodulator-naïve patients were randomized to infliximab, azathioprine, or both. At week 26, corticosteroid-free remission occurred in 56.8% of the combination group vs. 44.4% with infliximab alone and 30.0% with azathioprine alone, establishing combination therapy's superiority for induction/early maintenance in this naïve population.

**Adalimumab** — Three pivotal RCTs: **CLASSIC I** (induction, TNF-naïve, n=299), **CLASSIC II** (maintenance follow-on, n=55 re-randomized), and **CHARM** (maintenance, n=854 open-label induction → randomization of responders). In CLASSIC II, 79% of patients on adalimumab every-other-week and 83% weekly were in remission at week 56 vs. 44% on placebo. In CHARM, remission at week 26 was 40% (eow) and 47% (weekly) with adalimumab vs. 17% with placebo.

**Certolizumab pegol** — **PRECiSE 1** (induction, n=662) and **PRECiSE 2** (maintenance, n=425 responders re-randomized). Certolizumab produced a modest benefit in response rates at week 6 and weeks 6+26 versus placebo, though without a significant improvement in remission in PRECiSE 1, while PRECiSE 2 established maintenance efficacy in induction responders.

## Anti-integrin agents

**Vedolizumab (GEMINI 2)** — Integrated induction/maintenance trial; 368 patients were randomized to vedolizumab or placebo for induction, and 461 induction responders were randomized for the maintenance phase, establishing efficacy of the α4β7-integrin antibody in Crohn's disease.

**Natalizumab (ENACT-1/ENACT-2)** — ENACT-1 randomized patients 4:1 to natalizumab or placebo for induction, with responders at weeks 10 and 12 continuing into ENACT-2 for maintenance evaluation (note: natalizumab carries a PML risk that has limited its use in IBD).

## Anti-IL-12/23 and anti-IL-23 agents

**Ustekinumab (UNITI-1, UNITI-2, IM-UNITI)** — UNITI-1 (anti-TNF failures, n=741) and UNITI-2 (conventional-therapy failures, n=628) were induction trials; responders entered IM-UNITI, where 397 patients were randomized to ustekinumab every 8 or 12 weeks or placebo, with significantly more ustekinumab-treated patients in remission at week 44 than placebo (P≤0.003 for both regimens).

**Risankizumab (ADVANCE, MOTIVATE, FORTIFY)** — ADVANCE and MOTIVATE were randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled phase 3 induction trials in patients with inadequate response/intolerance to conventional therapy or biologics; FORTIFY was the phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled maintenance withdrawal trial in induction responders.

**Mirikizumab (VIVID-1)** — A 52-week phase 3, multicenter, randomized, double-blind trial comparing mirikizumab to placebo (and including a ustekinumab active-reference arm) in moderately-to-severely active Crohn's disease. By week 12, clinical response was 45.4% with mirikizumab vs. 19.6% with placebo, and by week 52 remission was 54.1% with mirikizumab.

## Small-molecule (JAK inhibitor)

**Upadacitinib (U-EXCEL, U-EXCEED, U-ENDURE)** — U-EXCEL and U-EXCEED randomized patients 2:1 to upadacitinib 45 mg or placebo for 12-week induction; responders entered U-ENDURE, randomized 1:1:1 to 15 mg, 30 mg upadacitinib, or placebo for 52-week maintenance. Clinical remission was significantly higher with upadacitinib in both induction trials (49.5% vs. 29.1% in U-EXCEL; 38.9% vs. 21.1% in U-EXCEED, P<0.001).
