Here's a set of individual primary RCTs (phase 2/3 trials) evaluating biologic and other advanced (targeted synthetic) therapies for induction or maintenance of remission in Crohn's disease. I've excluded reviews, meta-analyses, and guidelines, and organized them by drug class/mechanism.

- All items above are individual RCTs (phase 2/3 induction, maintenance, or head-to-head trials), not reviews or guidelines, per your inclusion criteria.
- Several agents have companion/replicate trials (e.g., risankizumab's ADVANCE + MOTIVATE, upadacitinib's U-EXCEL + U-EXCEED) that I've grouped together since they were reported in single or linked publications.
- This list is not exhaustive — for a formal evidence synthesis you'll want to supplement it with a systematic database search (MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP) to capture trial registrations, non-English-language publications, unpublished/negative trials, and more recent or smaller studies (e.g., filgotinib, etrasimod, newer anti-TNF biosimilar trials) that didn't surface in this targeted search.
