## Summary of the evidence

The pivotal trials fall into a few families, organized by drug/mechanism:

**Anti-TNF agents**
- Infliximab was the first biologic studied, established in a short-term induction trial recognized as the first-ever randomized controlled trial of infliximab in treating patients with Crohn's disease, in which patients were randomized to placebo or infliximab at doses of 5, 10, or 20 mg/kg, with the 5 mg/kg dose showing the best response and remission rates, though relapse was common by week 12. Maintenance efficacy was then shown in ACCENT I, and fistula-specific efficacy in a separate trial where a randomized, multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of infliximab was conducted in 94 adult patients with draining abdominal or perianal fistulas from Crohn's disease, followed by fistula maintenance data in a 306-patient trial (Sands 2004). The SONIC trial directly compared strategies: a randomized, double-blind trial evaluating the comparative efficacy and safety of infliximab and azathioprine, alone or in combination, for Crohn's disease, finding combination therapy superior to either monotherapy. A more recent NEJM Evidence trial examined stopping therapy after sustained remission, finding no relapses among 59 patients continuing infliximab, versus 23 of 56 patients experiencing relapse after discontinuing infliximab.
- Adalimumab has three key RCTs: CLASSIC I (299 patients naive to anti-TNF therapy), CHARM (499 patients receiving maintenance treatment), and GAIN (325 patients who had lost response to or could not tolerate infliximab).
- Certolizumab pegol was evaluated in PRECiSE 1, where 662 adults with moderate-to-severe Crohn's disease were randomly assigned to certolizumab pegol or placebo, with response rates at week 6 of 35% vs 27%, though remission rates did not differ significantly, and PRECiSE 2, a maintenance trial in responders.

**Gut-selective and IL-12/23/IL-23 agents**
- Vedolizumab's pivotal trial was GEMINI 2, published as "Vedolizumab as induction and maintenance therapy for Crohn's disease" in NEJM 2013.
- Ustekinumab's program included two induction trials and one maintenance trial: UNITI-1 and UNITI-2 (8-week induction trials) and IM-UNITI (44-week maintenance trial), together representing 52 weeks of therapy, with UNITI-1 enrolling 741 anti-TNF-refractory patients and UNITI-2 enrolling 628 conventional-therapy-refractory patients. An earlier phase 2b trial in anti-TNF-refractory patients also exists (Sandborn 2012).
- Risankizumab was tested in ADVANCE and MOTIVATE for induction, where ADVANCE and MOTIVATE were randomised, double-masked, placebo-controlled induction trials, and FORTIFY for maintenance, a phase 3, multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, withdrawal maintenance trial.

**JAK inhibitor**
- Upadacitinib's program comprised two induction trials (U-EXCEL and U-EXCEED) and one maintenance trial, with clinical remission rates significantly higher with 45-mg upadacitinib than placebo in both induction trials, and higher remission with 15-mg or 30-mg upadacitinib than placebo at week 52 in the maintenance trial. Filgotinib (DIVERSITY program) is another oral JAK inhibitor with completed phase 3 induction/maintenance RCTs.

I did not include natalizumab trials (ENACT-1/2) in the detailed pull above since search focus shifted, but they are established primary RCTs as well and can be added if you want the list expanded.
