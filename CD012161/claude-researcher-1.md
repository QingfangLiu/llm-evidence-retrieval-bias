A few notes before the list: these searches are illustrative rather than an exhaustive database search (MEDLINE/EMBASE/CENTRAL/ClinicalTrials.gov would be needed for a true systematic review), and I've excluded reviews, meta-analyses, and guidance documents per your request, keeping only individual trials.

**Comparisons covered:**
1. **Ultra-rapid analogues vs. earlier-generation rapid analogues** (faster aspart vs. insulin aspart; ultra-rapid lispro [URLi/Lyumjev] vs. lispro)
2. **First-generation rapid analogues (lispro, aspart) vs. regular human insulin**
3. **Related MDI-regimen comparisons** (e.g., analogue basal-bolus vs. NPH/regular combinations) that some reviewers include as indirect evidence

A general pattern across the newer trials: faster aspart and ultra-rapid lispro consistently show noninferiority (sometimes superiority) on HbA1c versus their predecessor analogues, with modest improvements in early postprandial glucose control, and largely similar overall hypoglycemia rates, though with more injection-site reactions in some URLi/faster aspart trials.
