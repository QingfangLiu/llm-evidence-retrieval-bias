**By treatment strategy:**

- **RATIONALE-315** (tislelizumab) — China-based

*Adjuvant-only ICI (after surgery ± adjuvant chemo):*
- **IMpower010** (atezolizumab)

*Single-arm phase 2 (foundational, non-randomized):*
