## Individual Primary Studies: Perioperative Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors in Resectable NSCLC

I searched for and identified randomized controlled trials (excluding systematic reviews, meta-analyses, narrative reviews, guidelines, and editorials) evaluating perioperative immune checkpoint inhibitors (neoadjuvant, adjuvant, or perioperative — alone or combined with chemotherapy) versus placebo/no ICI in patients with localized, resectable NSCLC. These are the trials underlying essentially all current evidence in this space; several also report age-stratified data relevant to older adults specifically, and one dedicated systematic review of the elderly subgroup (excluded from your list, but flagged below) draws on 8 of these RCTs.

**Neoadjuvant-only trials**
CheckMate 816 was an open-label international trial in which patients with resectable NSCLC were randomly assigned to neoadjuvant nivolumab plus chemotherapy or chemotherapy alone before surgery, resulting in significantly longer event-free survival and higher pathological complete response with nivolumab, without increasing adverse events or impeding surgical feasibility. A final analysis reported a 5-year overall survival rate of 65.4% with nivolumab plus chemotherapy versus 55.0% with chemotherapy alone. A related exploratory arm of the same trial compared neoadjuvant nivolumab plus ipilimumab and chemotherapy against chemotherapy alone in resectable NSCLC. NADIM II is an open-label, randomized, two-arm, multicenter phase 2 trial comparing neoadjuvant nivolumab plus chemotherapy against chemotherapy alone in resectable stage IIIA NSCLC.

**Perioperative (neoadjuvant + adjuvant) trials**
KEYNOTE-671 was a randomized, double-blind phase 3 trial in patients with resectable stage II–IIIB (N2) NSCLC randomized to neoadjuvant pembrolizumab or placebo with cisplatin-based chemotherapy, followed by surgery and adjuvant pembrolizumab or placebo. AEGEAN was a phase 3, international, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of 802 patients with resectable stage II–IIIB(N2) NSCLC randomized to durvalumab or placebo alongside platinum-based chemotherapy before surgery, followed by durvalumab or placebo after surgery. NEOTORCH was a randomized clinical trial comparing toripalimab combined with platinum-based chemotherapy versus chemotherapy alone on event-free survival and major pathological response in resectable stage III NSCLC. CheckMate 77T found that perioperative nivolumab added to neoadjuvant chemotherapy resulted in significantly longer event-free survival than perioperative placebo added to chemotherapy, with higher pathological complete response (25.3% vs 4.7%) and major pathological response (35.4% vs 12.1%).

**Adjuvant-only trials**
IMpower010 was a randomised, multicentre, open-label phase 3 trial that showed a disease-free survival benefit with adjuvant atezolizumab versus best supportive care after adjuvant chemotherapy in resected stage II–IIIA NSCLC, with pronounced benefit in PD-L1-positive tumors. PEARLS/KEYNOTE-091 was a randomised, triple-blind phase 3 trial comparing pembrolizumab with placebo as adjuvant therapy for completely resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC, finding significantly extended disease-free survival with pembrolizumab. CCTG BR.31 randomly assigned patients with resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC to durvalumab or placebo; adjuvant durvalumab did not improve disease-free survival versus placebo regardless of PD-L1 expression level. ANVIL (ALCHEMIST) was an open-label, randomized phase 3 study comparing adjuvant nivolumab against observation in resected NSCLC with any or ≥50% PD-L1 expression.
