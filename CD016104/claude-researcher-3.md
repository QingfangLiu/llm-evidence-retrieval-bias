## Summary of findings

**Landscape of primary evidence**

All major evidence in this space comes from a cluster of large, international phase 3 randomized controlled trials (RCTs) comparing ICI (± chemotherapy) against placebo/chemotherapy alone in resectable, locoregional (stage I–III) NSCLC, given neoadjuvantly, adjuvantly, or perioperatively (both). Several also have companion papers reporting elderly-specific subgroup or exploratory analyses.

- **Neoadjuvant-only ICI**: CheckMate 816 (nivolumab + chemo vs. chemo, stage IB–IIIA) was the first phase 3 trial to show benefit, with pathological complete response (pCR) and event-free survival (EFS) gains, and — in its 2025 final analysis — a significant overall survival (OS) benefit.
- **Perioperative ICI (neoadjuvant + adjuvant)**: KEYNOTE-671 (pembrolizumab), CheckMate 77T (nivolumab), AEGEAN (durvalumab), and Neotorch (toripalimab) all showed improved EFS, pCR/major pathological response, and (for KEYNOTE-671 and CheckMate 816) OS, versus chemotherapy plus placebo.
- **Adjuvant-only ICI**: IMpower010 (atezolizumab) and KEYNOTE-091/PEARLS (pembrolizumab) evaluated ICI after surgery ± chemotherapy versus placebo/best supportive care, with disease-free survival (DFS) benefit, particularly in PD-L1-high subgroups.
- **IMpower030** (neoadjuvant atezolizumab) is a further phase 3 trial in this space; its primary publication should be checked directly for full results, as available sources here mainly show trial-design/protocol papers.

Notably, dedicated *primary* trials restricted to older adults specifically in the perioperative NSCLC setting are scarce — most age-specific evidence exists as **subgroup or exploratory analyses nested within these larger RCTs** (e.g., a Journal of Clinical Oncology subgroup abstract of KEYNOTE-091 by treatment status, and various trial subgroup analyses by age ≥65 or ≥75 reported in conference proceedings/secondary papers tied to the trials below), rather than as standalone elderly-focused primary studies. If you need those age-specific subgroup data points extracted trial-by-trial (rather than the parent trial), that would be a good candidate for a deeper literature-mapping pass, since ASCO/ESMO abstracts with age-stratified hazard ratios are scattered across many conference proceedings not fully indexed in standard databases.
