Here's a summary of the primary randomized controlled trial evidence on immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) used around lung cancer surgery, comparing them with placebo or chemotherapy alone.

## What "perioperative" ICI trials generally test

These trials fall into three designs:
- **Neoadjuvant-only**: ICI + chemo before surgery, nothing extra after
- **Perioperative**: ICI + chemo before surgery, then ICI alone after surgery
- **Adjuvant-only**: ICI (or placebo) given only after surgery, without a pre-surgery immunotherapy component

**Neoadjuvant-only:** CheckMate 816 randomly assigned 358 patients with stage IB to IIIA resectable NSCLC to neoadjuvant nivolumab plus platinum-doublet chemotherapy or chemotherapy alone for three cycles before surgery. Neoadjuvant nivolumab plus chemotherapy resulted in significantly longer event-free survival and a higher percentage of patients with a pathological complete response than chemotherapy alone, without increasing adverse events or impeding surgery. A 2025 final analysis showed this translated into a nearly 30% reduction in the risk of death compared with chemotherapy alone.

**Perioperative (pre- and post-surgery) trials:**
- **KEYNOTE-671** (pembrolizumab): a randomized, double-blind, phase 3 trial evaluating perioperative pembrolizumab in patients with early-stage NSCLC. Neoadjuvant pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy followed by resection and adjuvant pembrolizumab significantly improved event-free survival, major pathological response, and pathological complete response compared with neoadjuvant chemotherapy alone followed by surgery. A 5-year update showed OS of 64.6% vs 53.6% and EFS of 49.9% vs 26.5%.
- **AEGEAN** (durvalumab): a phase 3, international, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial investigating durvalumab given perioperatively (neoadjuvant and adjuvant) along with neoadjuvant chemotherapy in resectable NSCLC. 802 patients with stage II to IIIB disease were randomly assigned to neoadjuvant chemotherapy plus durvalumab or placebo, followed by adjuvant durvalumab or placebo, with significant improvement in both event-free survival and pathologic complete response.
- **CheckMate 77T** (nivolumab): a trial showing significant improvements in event-free survival with perioperative nivolumab, consistent with other reports of perioperative immunotherapy in resectable NSCLC.
- **NEOTORCH** (toripalimab): the results showed that compared to perioperative chemotherapy alone, toripalimab in combination with chemotherapy as perioperative treatment led to a significant improvement in event-free survival, reducing the risk of disease recurrence, progression, or death by 60%. It also significantly reduced the surgery cancellation rate (17.8% vs. 26.7%).

**Adjuvant-only trials (surgery first, ICI or placebo only afterward):**
- **IMpower010** (atezolizumab): a randomised, multicentre, open-label, phase 3 study comparing adjuvant atezolizumab versus best supportive care after adjuvant platinum-based chemotherapy, showing a disease-free survival benefit, with pronounced benefit in patients whose tumors expressed PD-L1 on 1% or more of tumor cells.
- **PEARLS/KEYNOTE-091** (pembrolizumab): a randomized, triple-blind phase 3 trial that significantly improved disease-free survival compared with placebo, without new safety signals, in completely resected, PD-L1-unselected, stage IB–IIIA NSCLC. Median DFS was 53.6 months with pembrolizumab vs 42.0 months with placebo, though benefit in the PD-L1 <1% subgroup was less clear.
