Here's an overview of individual primary studies (randomized controlled trials) comparing recombinant FSH (rFSH) with other gonadotropin preparations for ovarian stimulation in IVF/ICSI.

## rFSH vs. highly purified human menopausal gonadotropin (HP-hMG)

**Andersen, Devroey & Arce (2006), Human Reproduction** — A multinational, randomized, assessor-blind trial in 731 women following a long GnRH agonist protocol. More oocytes were retrieved after rFSH treatment (11.8) compared with HP-hMG treatment (10.0), but a higher proportion developed into top-quality embryos with HP-hMG. The ongoing pregnancy rate per cycle was 27% with HP-hMG and 22% with rFSH, and superiority of HP-hMG could not be concluded, but non-inferiority was established.

**Bosch, Vidal, Labarta, Simon, Remohi & Pellicer (2008), Human Reproduction** — Randomized study comparing highly purified hMG with rFSH specifically in a GnRH antagonist ovarian hyperstimulation protocol.

**Ye, Huang, Pei, Zeng & Luo (2012), Gynecological Endocrinology** — A prospective randomized controlled trial in 127 normogonadotropic women ≥35 years old (advanced reproductive age) undergoing their first IVF/ICSI cycle, comparing HP-hMG (n=63) with rFSH (n=64) in a long GnRH agonist protocol. More leading follicles and oocytes were obtained in the rFSH group.

## rFSH vs. corifollitropin alfa (long-acting rFSH)

**Devroey, Boostanfar, Koper, Mannaerts, IJzerman-Boon & Fauser — ENGAGE trial (2009), Human Reproduction** — A phase-3, randomized, double-blind, active-controlled, non-inferiority clinical trial investigating a single injection of corifollitropin alfa compared with daily rFSH in 1506 women weighing 60–90 kg using a GnRH antagonist protocol.

**Corifollitropin Alfa ENSURE Study Group (2010), Reproductive BioMedicine Online** — Randomized trial in lower-body-weight women (≤60 kg) comparing corifollitropin alfa with rFSH.

**PURSUE trial** — Randomized comparison of corifollitropin alfa (150 µg) vs. rFSH (300 IU) in women aged 35–42.

**Follow-up pharmacokinetic/dynamic report of the ENGAGE data (2010), Reproductive BioMedicine Online** — A double-blind randomized controlled trial assessing pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of 150μg corifollitropin alfa versus daily 200IU rFSH in 1509 patients.

## rFSH vs. follitropin delta (a distinct rFSH molecule)

**ADAPT-1 trial — Bernabeu, Zajc, García Sánchez, Agrawal, Papaleo, Jirecek, Møgelmose, Jepsen & Lobo (2025), Human Reproduction** — A randomized, controlled, open-label, assessor-blinded, multicentre trial comparing follitropin delta 15 µg/day with follitropin alfa 225 IU/day in conventional dosing regimens in women aged 18–40 undergoing ovarian stimulation for IVF/ICSI.

**Repeated-cycle safety trial (2018/2019), Reproductive BioMedicine Online / PubMed** — A controlled, assessor-blind Phase 3 trial in IVF/ICSI patients undergoing repeated cycles (2 and 3) of stimulation with follitropin delta or follitropin alfa, evaluating immunogenicity (anti-FSH antibodies) and clinical outcomes across cycles.
