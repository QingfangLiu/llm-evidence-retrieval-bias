**Overview of what the primary literature covers**

The comparisons cluster into three main categories:

1. **rFSH vs. urinary/highly-purified hMG** — the largest body of trials, including the multinational MERIT trial which found ongoing pregnancy rates of 27% with HP-hMG versus 22% with rFSH, with superiority of HP-hMG not established but non-inferiority confirmed, and the Dutch multicenter trial where ongoing pregnancy rates were 26.3% and 25.2% for highly purified hMG and rFSH respectively, with no statistically significant difference. Trials specifically in PCOS populations were also found, with one showing significantly lower peak estradiol, fewer intermediate follicles, and fewer oocytes retrieved in the hMG group, with no difference in pregnancy rates between groups, and OHSS occurring in 11.9% of the rFSH group versus none in the hMG group.

2. **rFSH alone vs. rFSH + recombinant LH (r-hLH)** — several trials test whether adding LH activity to FSH-only stimulation improves outcomes, particularly in poor responders. The ESPART trial compared a fixed-ratio combination of follitropin alfa plus lutropin alfa against r-hFSH monotherapy in 939 women with poor ovarian response, finding primary and secondary efficacy endpoints were comparable between the two groups. An earlier trial in patients with inadequate response found that rLH supplementation was more effective than simply increasing the rFSH dose, with significantly more oocytes retrieved in the rLH-supplemented group.

3. **hMG vs. rFSH+rLH** (comparing two different sources of LH activity) — for example, a real-world cohort of 848 IVF patients classified as poor or normal responders, comparing rFSH+rLH (2:1 ratio) against hMG, with pregnancy rates analyzed by number of oocytes retrieved.

### Primary studies identified

1. Bahçeci M, et al. (or similar authors — Zekai Tahir Burak Hospital). *Human Menopausal Gonadotropin versus Recombinant FSH in Polycystic Ovary Syndrome Patients Undergoing In Vitro Fertilization.* Prospective RCT, n=80. PMC3850313.

2. *Randomized Controlled Trial Comparing Embryonic Quality in rFSH Versus hMG in IVF Protocol With GnRH Antagonist.* RCT, n=188. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02412904.

3. Andersen AN, Devroey P, Arce JC (MERIT Group). *Clinical outcome following stimulation with highly purified hMG or recombinant FSH in patients undergoing IVF: a randomized assessor-blind controlled trial.* Hum Reprod. 2006;21(12):3217–3227.

4. *Introduce an optimal method of ovarian stimulation in the polycystic ovarian syndrome affected: a randomized controlled trial.* RCT, n=120 (r-FSH vs. u-HMG, minimal/mild OS). PMC10283261.

5. Westergaard LG, et al. *Efficacy and safety of highly purified menotropin versus recombinant follicle-stimulating hormone in in vitro fertilization/intracytoplasmic sperm injection cycles: a randomized, comparative trial.* Multinational phase III RCT, n=781. PMID 12215327.

6. Bosch E, et al. (or similar). *Effectiveness of highly purified human menopausal gonadotropin vs. recombinant follicle-stimulating hormone in first-cycle in vitro fertilization-intracytoplasmic sperm injection patients.* Dutch multicenter RCT, n=629. PMID 17681325.

7. Bjercke S, Tanbo T, Abyholm T, Omland A, Opøien HK, Fedorcsak P. *Clinical outcome following stimulation with highly purified hMG or recombinant FSH in patients undergoing their first treatment cycle of IVF or ICSI.* Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand. 2010;89(8):1053–60.

8. De Placido G, et al. *Recombinant human LH supplementation versus recombinant human FSH (rFSH) step-up protocol during controlled ovarian stimulation in normogonadotrophic women with initial inadequate ovarian response to rFSH.* Multicentre prospective RCT. Hum Reprod. 2005;20(2):390–396.

9. Humaidan P, Chin W, Rogoff D, D'Hooghe T, Longobardi S, Hubbard J, Schertz J (ESPART Study Investigators). *Efficacy and safety of follitropin alfa/lutropin alfa in ART: a randomized controlled trial in poor ovarian responders.* Hum Reprod. 2017;32(3):544–555. PMC5850777.

10. Pacchiarotti A, Sbracia M, Frega A, et al. *Urinary hMG (Meropur) versus recombinant FSH plus recombinant LH (Pergoveris) in IVF: a multicenter, prospective, randomized controlled trial.* Fertil Steril. 2010;94(6):2467–2469.

11. *Randomized trial to compare the effect of recombinant human FSH (follitropin alfa) with or without recombinant human LH in women undergoing assisted reproduction treatment.* RBMO. 2004; (r-hFSH ± r-hLH, ICSI male-factor infertility, n=431).

12. *HMG versus recombinant FSH plus recombinant LH in ovarian stimulation for IVF: does the source of LH preparation matter?* RCT reported in RBMO, 2019.
