Going through IVF is a profound and demanding process, and taking a close look at your hormone protocol is a smart way to advocate for yourself. When it comes to stimulating your ovaries to produce multiple follicles, reproductive endocrinologists have a few different gonadotropin options.

Here is a breakdown of how recombinant follicle-stimulating hormone (rFSH) compares to the most common alternative, highly purified human menopausal gonadotropin (HP-hMG), based strictly on primary studies (randomized controlled trials and their direct comparative analyses).

## The Core Difference: Pure FSH vs. FSH + LH

The fundamental difference between these medications lies in how they are manufactured and their resulting hormone profile:

* **rFSH (e.g., Gonal-f, Follistim):** Created in a laboratory using recombinant DNA technology. It delivers 100% pure follicle-stimulating hormone to drive follicle growth.
* **HP-hMG (e.g., Menopur):** Derived and highly purified from the urine of postmenopausal women. It contains a combination of FSH and Luteinizing Hormone (LH) activity.

---

## What Primary Studies Reveal

While both medications successfully stimulate the ovaries, individual primary studies highlight a few key nuances in how they perform during a treatment cycle:

### 1. Egg Quantity vs. Embryo Quality

Because rFSH is heavily concentrated and purely focused on stimulation, it can sometimes produce a more aggressive initial response. A randomized controlled trial by Chapon et al. (2020) observed that rFSH tends to retrieve a higher total number of oocytes (eggs) compared to hMG. However, the study noted that the presence of LH in the hMG protocol influences the follicle maturation and natural atresia process. As a result, while hMG may yield fewer total eggs, the resulting embryonic quality is often comparable, and sometimes even superior, to rFSH (Chapon et al., 2020).

### 2. Pregnancy and Live Birth Rates

Despite potential differences in the exact number of eggs retrieved, the ultimate goal is a healthy pregnancy. Primary studies consistently show that clinical pregnancy rates, fertilization rates, and cycle cancellation rates are remarkably similar between rFSH and HP-hMG protocols. For instance, a trial examining patients undergoing Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI) found that HP-hMG is just as effective as rFSH in achieving clinical pregnancy and yielding similar oocyte maturity rates (Dalal, 2012).

### 3. Safety (OHSS) and Efficiency in High Responders

If your doctor predicts you will have a high ovarian response (usually indicated by a high AMH level or antral follicle count), the choice of gonadotropin can impact safety. An analysis based on the MEGASET-HR trial demonstrated that high-responder patients treated with HP-hMG required fewer embryo transfers to achieve similar live birth rates compared to those treated with rFSH (Khair et al., 2023). Furthermore, the HP-hMG group experienced a significantly lower rate of Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS), making it a safer and often more cost-effective option for women at high risk of over-responding to stimulation (Khair et al., 2023).
