I now have a solid set of individual primary studies (RCTs) directly comparing recombinant FSH with other gonadotropins for ovarian stimulation in IVF/ICSI. Below is a summary followed by the reference list, with the systematic reviews/meta-analyses I encountered along the way deliberately excluded.

## Summary of findings

**rFSH vs. highly purified hMG (HP-hMG)** — the most heavily studied comparison. Results are mixed: some trials (Andersen 2006; the pooled Devroey/Andersen/Bergh integrated analysis 2008) found higher live birth rates with HP-hMG, attributed to its LH/hCG activity; others (Bosch 2008 in antagonist cycles; the large MEGASET trial 2012; MEGASET-HR in high responders, Witz 2020) found comparable efficacy but generally fewer oocytes and sometimes lower OHSS/pregnancy-loss rates with HP-hMG. rFSH consistently yields more oocytes retrieved.

**rFSH vs. rFSH + rLH (LH supplementation)** — Benefit of adding LH appears concentrated in poor responders (Levi-Setti 2005 rLH step-up trial; the large Pergoveris/ESPART-type poor-responder trial), while less clear in unselected populations.

**hMG (urinary) vs. rFSH+rLH combination** — Pacchiarotti 2010 found comparable outcomes between urinary hMG and the recombinant FSH/LH combination.

**rFSH vs. urinary FSH** — Older trials (e.g., Mohamed 2006) suggested urinary FSH could outperform rFSH in older women, though this is a less consistently replicated finding.

Overall, individual RCTs show no consistent superiority of one gonadotropin type across all populations — differences (oocyte number, embryo quality, OHSS, pregnancy/live birth rates) vary by patient population (poor vs. normal vs. high responders) and stimulation protocol (agonist vs. antagonist).

## Primary studies (RCTs)

1. **Kilani Z, Dakkak A, Ghunaim S, Cognigni GE, Tabarelli C, Parmegiani L, Filicori M.** A prospective, randomized, controlled trial comparing highly purified hMG with recombinant FSH in women undergoing ICSI: ovarian response and clinical outcomes. *Hum Reprod.* 2003;18(6):1194–1199.

2. **Andersen AN, Devroey P, Arce JC.** Clinical outcome following stimulation with highly purified hMG or recombinant FSH in patients undergoing IVF: a randomized assessor-blind controlled trial. *Hum Reprod.* 2006;21(12):3217–3227.

3. **Andersen AN, et al. (integrated analysis of two RCTs).** Highly purified HMG versus recombinant FSH for ovarian stimulation in IVF cycles. *Reprod Biomed Online.* 2008;17(4):500–505 (also *Fertil Steril* companion publication).

4. **Bosch E, Vidal C, Labarta E, Simon C, Remohi J, Pellicer A.** Highly purified hMG versus recombinant FSH in ovarian hyperstimulation with GnRH antagonists—a randomized study. *Hum Reprod.* 2008;23(10):2346–2351.

5. **Ye H, Huang G, Pei L, Zeng P, Luo X.** Outcome of in vitro fertilization following stimulation with highly purified hMG or recombinant FSH in downregulated women of advanced reproductive age: a prospective, randomized and controlled trial. *Gynecol Endocrinol.* 2012;28(7):540–544.

6. **Devroey P, Pellicer A, Nyboe Andersen A, Arce JC; MEGASET Trial Group.** A randomized assessor-blind trial comparing highly purified hMG and recombinant FSH in a GnRH antagonist cycle with compulsory single-blastocyst transfer. *Fertil Steril.* 2012;97(3):561–571.

7. **Witz CA, Daftary GS, Doody KJ, Park JK, Seifu Y, Yankov VI, Heiser PW; MEGASET-HR Trial Group.** Randomized, assessor-blinded trial comparing highly purified human menotropin and recombinant follicle-stimulating hormone in high responders undergoing intracytoplasmic sperm injection (MEGASET-HR). *Fertil Steril.* 2020;114(2):321–330.

8. **Levi-Setti PE, Cavagna M, Bulletti C.** Recombinant human LH supplementation versus recombinant human FSH (rFSH) step-up protocol during controlled ovarian stimulation in normogonadotrophic women with initial inadequate ovarian response to rFSH: a multicentre, prospective, randomized controlled trial. *Hum Reprod.* 2005;20(2):390–396.

9. **Pacchiarotti A, Sbracia M, Frega A, Selman H, Rinaldi L, Pacchiarotti A.** Urinary hMG (Meropur) versus recombinant FSH plus recombinant LH (Pergoveris) in IVF: a multicenter, prospective, randomized controlled trial. *Fertil Steril.* 2010;94(6):2467–2469.

10. **Mohamed MA, Sbracia M, Pacchiarotti A, et al.** Urinary follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) is more effective than recombinant FSH in older women in a controlled randomized study. *Fertil Steril.* 2006;85(5):1398–1403.

11. **(Poor responders, early vs. late LH exposure)** — Prospective, randomized, controlled study of early vs. mid-follicular LH exposure (rFSH + rLH from day 1 vs. day 7) in patients with poor ovarian responsiveness. *J Assist Reprod Genet.* 2012.

12. **Phase III r-hFSH/r-hLH vs. r-hFSH trial in poor ovarian responders** — Single-blind, active-comparator, randomized, parallel-group trial of follitropin alfa/lutropin alfa vs. r-hFSH monotherapy (n=939) in patients with poor ovarian response (the ESPART-related trial). Published as: Efficacy and safety of follitropin alfa/lutropin alfa in ART: a randomized controlled trial in poor ovarian responders. *Hum Reprod* (or associated journal), 2017–2018.
