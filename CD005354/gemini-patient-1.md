Going through IVF is a profound and demanding experience, both emotionally and physically. The stimulation phase—getting your ovaries to safely produce multiple mature eggs—is a crucial step, and choosing the right medication is a big part of that process.

Recombinant follicle-stimulating hormone (**rFSH**) is produced synthetically in a lab. Because it is completely lab-created rather than biologically extracted, it is highly pure, easily standardized, and extremely reliable.

Here is how rFSH compares to other types of gonadotropin hormones based entirely on data from individual primary clinical trials:

## rFSH vs. Highly Purified Human Menopausal Gonadotropin (HP-hMG)

While rFSH provides pure follicle-stimulating hormone, HP-hMG is biologically purified from the urine of postmenopausal women and contains a combination of both FSH and luteinizing hormone (LH).

* **Overall Efficacy:** Primary randomized controlled trials show no statistically significant differences in the ultimate goals: ongoing pregnancy rates and live birth rates (Andersen et al., 2006; Bosch et al., 2008).
* **Oocyte Yield:** Women taking rFSH often retrieve slightly more oocytes (eggs) overall, but the final number of high-quality embryos and successful pregnancies tends to remain comparable between the two groups (Bosch et al., 2008).
* **Safety for High Responders:** For women predicted to have a very high response to stimulation, protocols using HP-hMG have been associated with a slightly lower risk of developing Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS) compared to rFSH (Khair et al., 2023).

## rFSH vs. Urinary FSH (uFSH)

Like rFSH, uFSH is strictly follicle-stimulating hormone, but it is extracted and purified from urine rather than synthesized in a lab.

* **Comparable Outcomes:** A randomized trial comparing synthetic rFSH to highly purified uFSH found that clinical pregnancy rates and live birth rates remain directly comparable (Patki et al., 2018).
* **The Difference:** The main advantage of rFSH over uFSH isn't a difference in pregnancy success, but rather the consistency of a lab-synthesized product, which avoids the batch-to-batch variations sometimes seen with biological extraction.

## Daily rFSH vs. Corifollitropin alfa (Long-Acting rFSH)

Corifollitropin alfa is an extended-release version of rFSH. It interacts with your body the same way, but it stays in your system much longer.

* **Injection Burden:** A single injection of corifollitropin alfa can replace the first seven days of daily rFSH injections.
* **Efficacy & Safety:** A large, double-blind randomized trial found that the ongoing pregnancy rates and the safety profiles (including OHSS risk) resulting from this single sustained injection were functionally identical to those achieved with standard daily rFSH injections (Devroey et al., 2014).
