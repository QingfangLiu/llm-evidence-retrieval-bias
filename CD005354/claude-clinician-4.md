## Primary studies: rFSH vs. other gonadotropins for ovarian stimulation in IVF

**rFSH vs. highly purified/urinary hMG (HP-hMG)**

- Andersen AN, et al. *Clinical outcome following stimulation with highly purified hMG or recombinant FSH in patients undergoing IVF: a randomized assessor-blind controlled trial.* Hum Reprod. 2006;21(12):3217–27. — RCT, 731 women, long agonist protocol; rFSH gave more oocytes, HP-hMG a higher proportion of top-quality embryos; ongoing pregnancy rates similar.
- Devroey P, et al. (integrated analysis of two RCTs). *Highly purified HMG versus recombinant FSH for ovarian stimulation in IVF cycles.* Reprod Biomed Online. 2008. — Pooled raw data (n=986) from two RCTs; live birth rate significantly higher with HP-HMG (26.5% vs 20.8%).
- Bosch E, et al. (IVI Valencia). *HMG vs rFSH in GnRH antagonist cycles* (registered as NCT00669786). — RCT, 280 patients, antagonist protocol; no significant difference in ongoing pregnancy rate; rFSH group had more oocytes.
- Out HJ, et al. *Comparative effectiveness of recombinant FSHα versus highly purified hMG.* / Ferraretti et al. *A prospective, randomized, controlled trial comparing highly purified hMG with recombinant FSH in women undergoing ICSI.* PMID 12773445. — RCT, 100 women; HP-hMG associated with shorter treatment duration and lower gonadotropin dose; ICSI outcomes similar.
- Westergaard LG, Erb K, Laursen S, Rasmussen PE, Rex S. *The effect of human menopausal gonadotrophin and highly purified, urine-derived follicle stimulating hormone on the outcome of IVF in down-regulated normogonadotrophic women.* Hum Reprod. 1996;11(6):1209–13.
- Filicori M, Cognigni GE, Pocognoli P, Tabarelli C, Ferlini F, Perri T, Parmegiani L. *Comparison of controlled ovarian stimulation with human menopausal gonadotropin or recombinant follicle-stimulating hormone.* (RCT).
- Bahceci M, et al. *Human Menopausal Gonadotropin versus Recombinant FSH in Polycystic Ovary Syndrome Patients Undergoing In Vitro Fertilization.* — RCT, 80 women with PCOS; lower OHSS with hMG, similar pregnancy rates.

**rFSH vs. urinary FSH (uFSH)**

- Kelly AJ, et al. / Jones Institute group. *Clinical efficacy of highly purified urinary FSH versus recombinant FSH in volunteers undergoing controlled ovarian stimulation for IVF: a randomized, multicenter, investigator-blind trial.* — RCT, n=152; no significant differences in oocyte number, clinical pregnancy, or live birth rate.

**rFSH alone vs. rFSH + recombinant LH (rLH)**

- Vuong TNL, Phung HT, Ho MT. *Recombinant follicle-stimulating hormone and recombinant luteinizing hormone versus recombinant follicle-stimulating hormone alone during GnRH antagonist ovarian stimulation in patients aged ≥35 years: a randomized controlled trial.* Hum Reprod. 2015;30(5):1188–95. — RCT, 240 women.
- König TE, van der Houwen LEE, Overbeek A, et al. *Recombinant LH supplementation to a standard GnRH antagonist protocol in women of 35 years or older undergoing IVF/ICSI: a randomized controlled multicentre study.* Hum Reprod. 2013;28(10):2804–12. — RCT, 253 couples; no benefit of LH supplementation on pregnancy/implantation/ovarian response.
- Younis JS, Izhaki I, Ben-Ami M. *The effect of LH supplementation to the GnRH antagonist protocol in advanced reproductive ageing women: a prospective randomized controlled study.* Clin Endocrinol (Oxf). 2016;84:99–106.
- Bosch E, et al. 2011 (referenced/commented on by König et al., 2014) — RCT on rLH supplementation in GnRH antagonist cycles, poor/older responders.
- Gizzo S, Andrisani A, Noventa M, et al. *Recombinant LH supplementation during IVF cycles with a GnRH-antagonist in estimated poor responders: A cross-matched pilot investigation of the optimal daily dose and timing.* Mol Med Rep. 2015. — Prospective randomized cross-matched study, n=40.

**hMG vs. rFSH + rLH**

- Pacchiarotti A, Sbracia M, Frega A, Selman H, Rinaldi L, Pacchiarotti A. *Urinary hMG (Meropur) versus recombinant FSH plus recombinant LH (Pergoveris) in IVF: a multicenter, prospective, randomized controlled trial.* Fertil Steril. 2010;94(6):2467–9. — RCT, 122 patients; comparable outcomes, higher OHSS risk with rFSH+rLH.
- Tehraninejad ES, et al. (2017) — RCT comparing HP-hMG vs rFSH+rLH (cited in RBMO 2019 review as a second RCT alongside Pacchiarotti).

**Dose-finding / other rFSH comparator RCTs**

- Arce JC, Nyboe Andersen A, Fernández-Sánchez M, et al. *Ovarian response to recombinant human follicle-stimulating hormone: a randomized, antimüllerian hormone-stratified, dose-response trial in women undergoing IVF/ICSI.* Fertil Steril. 2014. — RCT, 265 women, comparing novel rhFSH (FE 999049) doses against standard follitropin alfa.
