## Primary studies identified: telepharmacy vs usual care for long-term conditions, non-hospital settings

I excluded reviews, meta-analyses, guidelines, and protocols-only records where a completed trial with results wasn't available. Grouped by condition area:

**Hypertension**
- Karaoui et al. (2021), *BMC Health Serv Res* — pharmacist telephone education/follow-up post-discharge (anticoagulation focus, but outcomes tracked in outpatient phase); RCT, n=200.
- Community pharmacist-led nationwide HBPM program with digital case management vs. traditional pharmacist support, Taiwan, n=1216 — a nationwide observational study comparing digital case management with telemonitoring versus traditional pharmacist-led support for 3 months in adults with or at increased risk of hypertension.
- CELESTE trial (NCT06108674) — RCT comparing telepharmacy vs. in-person pharmaceutical care for uncontrolled hypertension in older primary-care patients (currently recruiting, so no results yet — flag as ongoing).

**Diabetes**
- Wang et al. (2022), *Int J Clin Pract* — "Efficacy of Pharmaceutical Care in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Hypertension: a Randomized Controlled Trial."
- Gerber et al. (2023), *JAMA Netw Open* — RCT of 221 African American or Latinx adults with type 2 diabetes and HbA1c ≥8%, testing a clinical pharmacist– and health coach–delivered mHealth intervention; HbA1c fell by a mean 0.79 percentage points in the intervention arm vs 0.24 in the waitlist control.
- Pharmacist-led telephone intervention, Nigeria (Obafemi Awolowo University), *J Pharm Health Serv Res* — a randomized trial finding the pharmacist-led telephone intervention significantly improved medication refill adherence, glycemic control, knowledge, and self-reported adherence in type 2 diabetes patients.
- Gay et al. (2006), *Diabetes Metab* — RCT of pharmacist/telecare reinforced follow-up for children/adolescents with type 1 diabetes.

**Anticoagulation (community/ambulatory, non-hospital)**
- Alanazi et al. (2022), *Ann Saudi Med* — crossover study, virtual vs in-person anticoagulation clinic, time-in-therapeutic-range outcomes.
- Tele-pharmacy anticoagulation clinic during COVID-19, Saudi Arabia (PMID 34566632) — single-service evaluation of TTR and patient satisfaction (note: no concurrent usual-care comparator; borderline for your "vs usual care" criterion — flag for your inclusion judgment).

**Heart failure**
- Impact of Pharmacist Telehealth Comanagement for Heart Failure (JACC: Advances, 2025) — nonrandomized controlled study evaluating telehealth-delivered pharmacist comanagement for HFrEF versus usual care, assessing GDMT optimization and healthcare utilization, n=190 intervention/277 comparison.

**Asthma**
- EmPhAsIS trial (De Vera et al., 2014 protocol; results published separately) — pragmatic cluster-RCT of community pharmacies randomized to an SMS-based mHealth adherence intervention vs usual care for asthma patients on inhaled corticosteroids.
- Rural school-based MDI technique RCT via interactive video pharmacist counseling — small RCT with adolescents randomized to telepharmacy video counseling vs. manufacturer package inserts for inhaler technique.

**Depression**
- Pharmacist-psychiatrist collaborative education RCT, India (2024/2025) — outpatient psychiatry RCT on adherence and quality of life (note: this one is in-person collaborative care, not clearly "tele" — worth checking full text if telephone/remote component exists).
