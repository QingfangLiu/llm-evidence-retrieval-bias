I excluded reviews, meta-analyses, guidelines, and protocols without reported results, and flagged the few borderline items. All studies below report primary data (trials or cohort studies).

**Hypertension**
- Margolis KL et al. *Effect of home blood pressure telemonitoring and pharmacist management on blood pressure control: a cluster randomized clinical trial.* JAMA. 2013;310:46–56. ("Hyperlink 1" — pharmacist-led telephone care + home BP telemonitoring vs. usual primary care, n=450, 16 clinics)

**Chronic kidney disease**
- *A pharmacist based intervention to improve the care of patients with CKD: a pragmatic, randomized, controlled trial.* (Phone-based pharmacist intervention + pharmacist–physician collaboration vs. usual care, 13 VA primary care clinics, n=2,199) — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4405859/

**Type 2 diabetes**
- *Pharmacist-led intervention to improve treatment outcomes in type 2 diabetes: a randomized controlled trial.* J Pharm Health Serv Res. (Telephone-based pharmacist intervention; outcomes: refill adherence, glycemic control) — https://academic.oup.com/jphsr/article/15/2/rmae005/7634595

**Anticoagulation (long-term warfarin management)**
- Brasen CL et al. *Home Management of warfarin treatment through a real-time supervised telemedicine solution: a randomized controlled trial.* Telemed J E Health. 2019;25(2):109–15.
- Zhu Z et al. *New Internet-Based Warfarin Anticoagulation Management Approach After Mechanical Heart Valve Replacement: Prospective, Multicenter, Randomized Controlled Trial.* J Med Internet Res. 2021. — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8398748/
- *Efficacy, Safety, and Cost-Effectiveness of "Internet + Pharmacy Care" Via the Alfalfa App in Warfarin Therapy Management After Cardiac Valve Replacement: Randomized Controlled Trial.* (n=405, app-based pharmacist-led dosing vs. offline anticoagulation clinic) — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12112255/

**Heart failure**
- Murray MD et al. *Pharmacist intervention to improve medication adherence in heart failure: a randomized trial.* Ann Intern Med. 2007;146(10):714–25. (worth double-checking delivery mode — some pharmacist contact was home-visit/phone-based, not purely remote)
- *Utilizing Pharmacist-Led Telehealth Services in Ambulatory Patients with Heart Failure.* Innovations in Pharmacy. 2023. (Retrospective cohort, n=37, single-arm — not a comparative trial, but a primary study of telehealth pharmacist intervention outcomes) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10686676/
