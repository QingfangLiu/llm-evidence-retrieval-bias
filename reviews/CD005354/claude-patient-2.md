## Recombinant FSH (rFSH) vs. Other Gonadotropins — Summary

**1. rFSH vs. highly purified hMG (HP-hMG)**
Findings are mixed. One large RCT (Andersen et al., 2006, n=731) found HP-hMG non-inferior to rFSH, with more oocytes retrieved on rFSH but higher top-quality embryo rates on HP-hMG. A pooled integrated analysis of two RCTs comparing HP-HMG combining FSH and hCG-driven LH activity with rFSH alone found a live birth rate of 26.5% with HP-HMG versus 20.8% with rFSH (P = 0.041), favoring HP-HMG. A separate RCT in women ≥35 years found more leading follicles and oocytes with rFSH, though pregnancy outcomes were compared as well. A GnRH-antagonist-cycle RCT (Bosch et al., 2008) also compared the two.

**2. rFSH + rLH vs. rFSH alone**
Generally more relevant in poor responders. One RCT found rLH supplementation more effective than increasing the rFSH dose for ovarian outcomes in women with initial inadequate ovarian response to rFSH alone. The large ESPART trial (939 women) found comparable primary/secondary efficacy endpoints between fixed-ratio rFSH/rLH and rFSH monotherapy in poor responders. Another RCT examined early vs. mid-follicular LH exposure timing.

**3. rFSH + rLH vs. hMG**
A real-world/observational comparison (n=848) evaluated rFSH+rLH against hMG stratified by expected responder category. Other RCTs (e.g., Pacchiarotti et al. 2010, urinary hMG "Meropur" vs. Pergoveris) made similar head-to-head comparisons.

**4. Follitropin delta (new rFSH) vs. follitropin alfa (established rFSH)**
The ESTHER-1 trial (n=1,329) found ongoing pregnancy rates were similar between individualized follitropin delta and conventional follitropin alfa dosing (30.7% vs. 31.6%), with follitropin delta producing more women with a target 8–14 oocyte response and fewer excessive responses/OHSS-preventive measures. ESTHER-2 and ADAPT-1 further examined repeated-cycle immunogenicity and conventional dosing regimens.

**5. Corifollitropin alfa (long-acting rFSH) vs. daily rFSH**
The pivotal ENGAGE trial (n=1,506) and ENSURE/PURSUE trials compared a single sustained-release injection to daily rFSH injections, generally showing similar clinical outcomes with somewhat more oocytes retrieved on corifollitropin alfa.

## List of Primary Studies

4. **Bosch E, Vidal C, Labarta E, Simon C, Remohi J, Pellicer A.** Highly purified hMG versus recombinant FSH in ovarian hyperstimulation with GnRH antagonists—a randomized study. *Hum Reprod.* 2008;23(10):2346-2351.

5. **[Multicentre RCT]** Recombinant human LH supplementation versus recombinant human FSH (rFSH) step-up protocol during controlled ovarian stimulation in normogonadotrophic women with initial inadequate ovarian response to rFSH. *Hum Reprod.* 2005;20(2):390-396.

6. **[ESPART trial]** Efficacy and safety of follitropin alfa/lutropin alfa in ART: a randomized controlled trial in poor ovarian responders. (n=939, Phase III RCT).

7. **[Prospective, randomized, controlled study]** Outcome of in vitro fertilization in patients with proven poor ovarian responsiveness after early vs. mid-follicular LH exposure. *J Assist Reprod Genet.* (n=530).

8. **[Real-world/observational cohort]** Controlled Ovarian Stimulation with recombinant-FSH plus recombinant-LH vs. human Menopausal Gonadotropin based on the number of retrieved oocytes: results from a routine clinical practice in a real-life population. (n=848).

9. **Pacchiarotti A, Sbracia M, Frega A, Selman H, Rinaldi L, Pacchiarotti A.** Urinary hMG (Meropur) versus recombinant FSH plus recombinant LH (Pergoveris) in IVF: a multicenter, prospective, randomized controlled trial. *Fertil Steril.* 2010;94(6):2467-2469.

10. **Carone D, Caropreso C, Vitti A, Chiappetta R.** Efficacy of different gonadotropin combinations to support ovulation induction in WHO type I anovulation infertility: clinical evidence of human recombinant FSH/human recombinant LH in a 2:1 ratio and highly purified human menopausal gonadotropin. *J Endocrinol Invest.* 2012;35(11):996-1002.

11. **[ADAPT-1 trial]** Ovarian stimulation with follitropin delta for in vitro fertilization: a multicentre, randomized, assessor-blind comparison with follitropin alfa using conventional dosing regimens. *Hum Reprod.* 2025;40(9):1660.

12. **[ESTHER-1 Study Group]** Individualized versus conventional ovarian stimulation for in vitro fertilization: a multicenter, randomized, controlled, assessor-blinded, phase 3 noninferiority trial. *Fertil Steril.* 2017 (n=1,329).

13. **[ESTHER-2 Study Group; Bosch E, Havelock J, Sánchez Martin F, et al.]** Follitropin delta in repeated ovarian stimulation for IVF: a controlled, assessor-blind Phase 3 safety trial. (cycles 2 and 3, n=513/188).

14. **[BEYOND trial]** Randomized controlled trial comparing efficacy and safety of individualized follitropin delta dosing in a GnRH agonist versus antagonist protocol.

15. **[ENGAGE trial] Devroey P, Boostanfar R, Koper NP, Mannaerts BM, IJzerman-Boon PC, Fauser BC.** A double-blind, non-inferiority RCT comparing corifollitropin alfa and recombinant FSH during the first seven days of ovarian stimulation using a GnRH antagonist protocol. *Hum Reprod.* 2009;24:3063-3072 (also *Hum Reprod.* 2014;29:1116-1120).

16. **[Pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic sub-study of ENGAGE]** Pharmacokinetics and follicular dynamics of corifollitropin alfa versus recombinant FSH during ovarian stimulation for IVF. *Reprod Biomed Online.* 2010 (n=1,509).

17. **[ENSURE Study Group]** Corifollitropin alfa for ovarian stimulation in IVF: a randomized trial in lower-body-weight women. *Reprod Biomed Online.* 2010;21:66-76.

18. **[PURSUE trial]** Randomized trial of corifollitropin alfa vs. rFSH in women aged 35-42 years (referenced in individual patient data meta-analysis).

19. **Corifollitropin alfa Dose-Finding Study Group.** A randomized dose-response trial of a single injection of corifollitropin alfa to sustain multifollicular growth during controlled ovarian stimulation. *Hum Reprod.* 2008;23:2484-2492.
