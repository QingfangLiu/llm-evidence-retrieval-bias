Here's an overview of the primary literature comparing rFSH with other gonadotropin preparations (urinary hMG, highly purified hMG, urinary FSH, recombinant LH-containing regimens, and corifollitropin alfa) for ovarian stimulation in IVF. I excluded systematic reviews, meta-analyses, Cochrane reviews, narrative reviews, and guidelines, and focused on individual RCTs and cohort studies.

**rFSH vs. highly purified/urinary hMG**

The MERIT trial randomized 731 women to highly purified menotrophin (HP-hMG, n=363) or rFSH (n=368) following a long GnRH agonist protocol, finding more oocytes retrieved with rFSH but no significant difference in ongoing pregnancy rate. A related integrated analysis of two RCTs pooled raw data from HP-HMG (n=491) and rFSH (n=495) arms and found a significantly higher live birth rate with HP-HMG (26.5% vs 20.8%, P=0.041). A separate trial in PCOS patients randomized 80 women to hMG (n=38) or rFSH (n=42), finding lower peak estradiol, fewer oocytes, and lower coasting requirements with hMG, but no difference in pregnancy rates.

**rFSH vs. urinary FSH**

A randomized, investigator-blind trial of 152 IVF patients compared highly purified urinary FSH (n=76) with rFSH (n=76) at a 300 IU starting dose, finding no significant difference in oocytes retrieved, pregnancy, or live birth rates. In older patients, a randomized trial of 257 women over age 39 compared recombinant FSH (n=121) with urinary FSH (n=120), finding urinary FSH required significantly lower total gonadotropin dose and dose per oocyte, with no other significant differences. A retrospective cohort study also compared recombinant and urinary FSH across more than 2,000 GnRH antagonist cycles (Scientific Reports, 2019).

**rFSH combined with recombinant LH vs. other regimens**

Pacchiarotti et al. (2010) conducted a multicentre RCT comparing HMG with recombinant FSH plus recombinant LH (Pergoveris) in 122 patients under a long agonist protocol. Vuong et al. (2015) ran an RCT comparing rFSH+rLH versus rFSH alone in women aged ≥35 undergoing GnRH antagonist stimulation. An Iranian RCT of 140 patients compared rFSH plus HMG versus rFSH plus recombinant LH, assessing chemical/clinical pregnancy, live birth, and abortion rates. Bühler and Fischer conducted a matched case-control study comparing recombinant LH supplementation with urinary hCG-based LH activity, and Requena et al. compared the endocrine profile of rFSH+LH versus HP-hMG.

**Corifollitropin alfa (a long-acting rFSH) vs. daily rFSH/follitropin beta**

The pivotal ENGAGE trial was a randomized, double-blind, non-inferiority trial comparing corifollitropin alfa with recombinant FSH during the first seven days of stimulation using a GnRH antagonist protocol (Devroey et al., 2009), with 1,509 randomized patients aged 18–36 weighing >60 kg. The companion ENSURE trial randomized lower-body-weight women (≤60 kg) to 100 µg corifollitropin alfa (n=268) or 150 IU rFSH (n=128). The PURSUE trial was a large, comparative, randomized double-blind trial confirming non-inferiority of pregnancy rates for corifollitropin alfa compared with rFSH in a GnRH-antagonist protocol. In older/lower-weight Asian patients, a randomized trial conducted in Vietnam compared corifollitropin alfa 150 μg with follitropin beta 300 IU/day in women aged 35–42 years weighing ≥50 kg, finding equivalent oocyte yield and pregnancy/live birth rates. Another RCT compared corifollitropin alfa with follitropin beta specifically in poor responders undergoing ICSI. A separate economic analysis was conducted alongside an RCT of corifollitropin alfa versus follitropin beta. Post hoc/secondary analyses of the ENGAGE trial data have also examined treatment flexibility and follicular phase duration.

**Biosimilar rFSH vs. originator rFSH**

Rettenbacher et al. (2015) conducted a multicentre phase 3 study comparing the biosimilar Bemfola® with originator Gonal-f® in women undergoing ovarian stimulation for IVF.

**Follitropin alfa vs. follitropin beta**

A retrospective cohort study of 2,864 patients compared cumulative live birth rates between Puregon (follitropin beta, n=1,313) and Gonal-F (follitropin alfa, n=1,551) using propensity-score methods.
