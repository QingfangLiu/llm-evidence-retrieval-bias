## Summary of findings

The primary literature comparing rFSH to other gonadotropins (urinary hMG, highly purified hMG/menotropin, highly purified urinary FSH) for ovarian stimulation in IVF spans roughly two decades of RCTs, with mostly consistent conclusions:

- **rFSH vs. urinary hMG / highly purified hMG (HP-hMG)**: Most large multicenter RCTs (e.g., the MERIT trial, MEGASET trial) found comparable pregnancy and live-birth rates between the two, though a few individual trials and a pooled meta-analysis reported a modest live-birth advantage for hMG, attributed to its LH activity. Findings on oocyte number, OHSS, and embryo quality vary by trial and patient population (e.g., PCOS vs. normoresponders).
- **rFSH vs. highly purified urinary FSH (HP-hFSH)**: Trials generally show similar oocyte yield and pregnancy/live-birth rates, though some smaller studies report higher oocyte/embryo quality metrics favoring rFSH.
- **rFSH + rLH vs. hMG**: Comparable pregnancy rates, with differences mainly in oocyte quality metrics and total gonadotropin dose/cost.

Because individual trials differ in protocol (agonist vs. antagonist), patient population (normoresponder, poor responder, PCOS), and outcome definitions, the trials below should not be pooled informally — that's the type of synthesis a systematic review would normally handle, which you've excluded here by design.
