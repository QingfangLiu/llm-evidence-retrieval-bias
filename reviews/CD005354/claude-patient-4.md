## rFSH vs. Other Gonadotropins for Ovarian Stimulation in IVF — Primary Studies

**1. rFSH vs. highly purified urinary FSH (u-FSH-HP)**

- A four-center, investigator-blind RCT of 152 IVF patients found no significant difference in number of oocytes with highly purified urinary FSH (mean 16.3) compared with rFSH (mean 17.1), with similar total gonadotropin dose used.
- A 120-patient RCT in ICSI patients compared recombinant FSH (Gonal-F) versus highly purified urinary FSH (Metrodin-HP), with 60 patients per group, using a fixed FSH dose for 7 days before individualizing the dose based on ovarian response.

**2. rFSH vs. highly purified hMG (HP-hMG) — standard/agonist protocols**

- The large multinational MERIT trial randomized 731 women to HP-hMG (n=363) or rFSH (n=368) following a long GnRH agonist protocol. More oocytes were retrieved after rFSH treatment (11.8) than HP-hMG (10.0), but the ongoing pregnancy rate per cycle was 27% with HP-hMG versus 22% with rFSH, and non-inferiority of HP-hMG to rFSH was established.

**3. rFSH vs. HP-hMG — GnRH antagonist protocols**

- Bosch et al. conducted a randomized study comparing highly purified hMG versus recombinant FSH in ovarian hyperstimulation with GnRH antagonists, finding a similar outcome overall but some differences in oocyte yield and hormonal profile (Hum Reprod 2008).
- The MEGASET trial, a randomized assessor-blind trial in 749 patients with compulsory single-blastocyst transfer, found an ongoing pregnancy rate of 30% with HP-hMG versus 27% with rFSH (fresh cycle), and cumulative live birth including frozen cycles of 40% vs. 38%, establishing non-inferiority of HP-hMG to rFSH, with significant pharmacodynamic differences between preparations.
- Witz et al. ran a randomized, assessor-blinded trial comparing highly purified human menotropin and recombinant FSH specifically in high responders undergoing ICSI (Fertil Steril 2020).
- Ye et al. conducted a prospective RCT on outcome of IVF following stimulation with highly purified hMG or recombinant FSH in down-regulated women of advanced reproductive age (Gynecol Endocrinol 2012).

**4. rFSH vs. hMG in PCOS patients**

- An 80-patient RCT in PCOS women (long GnRH agonist protocol) randomized to hMG (n=38) or rFSH (n=42) found significantly lower peak estradiol, fewer intermediate follicles, and fewer oocytes/MII oocytes in the hMG group, no difference in fertilization or pregnancy rates, and OHSS occurred in 11.9% of rFSH patients versus 0% of hMG patients.
- A 120-patient four-arm RCT in PCOS patients using a GnRH-antagonist protocol compared minimal- and mild-stimulation with either r-FSH or u-HMG, finding significant differences among groups in stimulation duration, oocytes retrieved, and embryos obtained, but no differences in fertilization or implantation rates.

**5. rFSH vs. corifollitropin alfa (a long-acting recombinant FSH variant)**

- The ENGAGE trial, a phase-3 double-blind, double-dummy non-inferiority RCT of 1,506–1,509 women, compared a single 150 µg dose of corifollitropin alfa to daily 200 IU rFSH for the first 7 stimulation days, reporting ongoing pregnancy rates of 38.9% for corifollitropin alfa versus 38.1% for rFSH, a non-significant difference of 0.9%, with similar rates of follicular development between arms.
- The ENSURE trial, a double-blind, double-dummy equivalence RCT in 396 lower-body-weight women (2:1 randomization), compared 100 µg corifollitropin alfa to daily 150 IU rFSH and found 13.3 oocytes retrieved with corifollitropin alfa versus 10.6 with rFSH, a treatment difference of +2.5 oocytes favoring corifollitropin alfa, within the predefined equivalence margin.
- A multicenter RCT specifically in young poor ovarian responders (Bologna criteria) compared corifollitropin alfa followed by HP-hMG against daily rFSH, finding that corifollitropin alfa plus hp-HMG was not superior to daily 300 IU rFSH in a GnRH antagonist protocol, with comparable ongoing pregnancy rates between groups, though more supernumerary cryopreserved embryos were obtained with the corifollitropin alfa/hp-HMG regimen.
