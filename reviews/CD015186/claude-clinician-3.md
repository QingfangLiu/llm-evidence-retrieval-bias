## Overview

SC-MIGS (procedures acting on Schlemm's canal — trabecular micro-bypass stents, ab interno trabeculotomy/goniotomy, canaloplasty, and combined viscodilation-trabeculotomy) generally lower IOP and reduce medication burden in mild-to-moderate open-angle glaucoma, with a favorable safety profile compared to filtering surgery, but effect sizes vary by device and evidence quality ranges from large industry-sponsored RCTs to small case series.

**iStent / iStent inject (trabecular micro-bypass)**
- A 5-year randomized trial (n=101) compared two trabecular micro-bypass stents versus travoprost as initial treatment for newly diagnosed POAG.
- The iStent inject pivotal RCT found clinically and statistically greater IOP reduction without medication after iStent inject implantation combined with cataract surgery versus cataract surgery alone, with good safety through 2 years.
- A prospective, uncontrolled interventional case series (n=72) evaluated first-generation iStent effectiveness by baseline IOP over 24 months.

**Hydrus Microstent**
- The HORIZON study was a prospective, multicenter, single-masked RCT randomizing subjects 2:1 to Hydrus Microstent plus phacoemulsification versus phacoemulsification alone, showing superior IOP and medication reduction at 24 months, with published 3-year and 5-year follow-ups (3-year and 5-year outcomes reported).
- A US-cohort analysis of HORIZON followed 219 microstent and 112 phaco-only eyes for 24 months.

**Trabectome (ab interno trabeculotomy)**
- A prospective, non-randomized comparative trial (n=112) compared Trabectome+phacoemulsification versus trabeculectomy+phacoemulsification.
- A prospective nonrandomized cohort study (825 cases) compared ab interno trabeculectomy outcomes in exfoliation glaucoma versus POAG.
- A multicenter retrospective study evaluated 2-year Trabectome efficacy/safety in Chinese POAG patients.
- A single-surgeon prospective nonrandomized analysis examined Trabectome outcomes in eyes with preoperative IOP ≥30 mmHg.

**Gonioscopy-Assisted Transluminal Trabeculotomy (GATT)**
- A prospective RCT compared circumferential (360°) versus hemi (180°) GATT in primary congenital glaucoma.
- A study of 124 consecutive POAG cases followed GATT alone versus GATT combined with phacoemulsification for 24 months.
- A retrospective multicenter before-and-after study (270 eyes) evaluated GATT outcomes stratified by glaucoma severity.
- A retrospective chart review of 126 eyes assessed GATT success and complications across open- and closed-angle glaucomas.
- An ongoing RCT protocol (BANG vs. GATT) is comparing bent ab interno needle goniotomy to GATT in POAG.

**Kahook Dual Blade (excisional goniotomy)**
- A 12-month interventional, randomized, controlled clinical study evaluated ab interno trabeculectomy with the Kahook Dual Blade, and a related trial compared excisional goniotomy versus trabecular microbypass stent implantation in a prospective RCT in mild-to-moderate open-angle glaucoma (Falkenberry et al., J Cataract Refract Surg 2020).
- A randomized clinical trial compared trabecular meshwork morphological changes between Kahook Dual Blade goniotomy and microhook ab interno trabeculotomy.
- A prospective, non-randomized case series tracked 24-month IOP/medication outcomes of combined phaco-KDB across glaucoma subtypes.
- A retrospective comparison (315 eyes) compared 12-month outcomes of phaco-KDB versus phaco-iStent.

**Ab interno canaloplasty / OMNI (viscodilation + trabeculotomy)**
- A retrospective observational study (80 eyes, two sites) evaluated 360° Schlemm's canal viscodilation with OMNI, alone or combined with phacoemulsification.
- A prospective, non-randomized comparison study assessed superior versus inferior canaloplasty/trabeculotomy with OMNI.
- A retrospective single-practice series compared standalone versus combined ab interno canaloplasty and 180° versus 360° treatment extent.
- A prospective study (44 eyes) examined ciliochoroidal detachment after 360° suture trabeculotomy ab interno over 12 months.

**Head-to-head device comparisons**
- The COMPARE study was a prospective, multicenter, randomized, single-masked trial (152 eyes) comparing standalone Hydrus versus 2-iStent devices, finding higher surgical success and fewer medications with Hydrus, with similar safety profiles.
- The INTEGRITY study was a prospective randomized trial comparing iStent infinite versus Hydrus at 6 months, and a separate comparative study examined 2-year outcomes for Hydrus versus iStent inject combined with cataract surgery.
