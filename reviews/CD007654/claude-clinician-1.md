## Summary of findings

**GLP-1/GIP receptor agonists** consistently reduce systolic blood pressure (roughly 3–11 mmHg depending on dose and baseline BP) alongside weight loss, and in patients with established cardiovascular disease, semaglutide reduces major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) — with benefit seen regardless of hypertension history. Kidney outcomes (a common target-organ complication of hypertension) also improved with semaglutide in the SELECT trial.

**Phentermine/topiramate** reduces both office and 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure in patients with and without treated hypertension, without blunting the effect at higher levels of background antihypertensive therapy, and improves broader cardiovascular risk markers.

**Orlistat** produced modest but statistically significant reductions in blood pressure alongside weight loss and diabetes-incidence reduction over 4 years in a large obese cohort with normal or impaired glucose tolerance.

A caveat worth flagging to your patients/colleagues: most of these trials enrolled patients across a BMI/comorbidity spectrum rather than being restricted specifically to hypertensive populations, though several (SELECT, CONQUER's cardiovascular-risk sub-analysis, the ABPM substudies, and the IPD analysis of STEP trials) specifically stratified or subset by hypertension status or baseline BP.
