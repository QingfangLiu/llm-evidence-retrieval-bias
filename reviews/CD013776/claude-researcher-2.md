## Primary studies: Blue-light (photodynamic-diagnosis, PDD/HAL) vs. white-light TURBT for NMIBC

**Large multicenter/pivotal RCTs**

1. **Heer R, Lewis R, Vadiveloo T, et al.** "A Randomized Trial of PHOTOdynamic Surgery in Non–Muscle-Invasive Bladder Cancer" (**PHOTO trial**). *NEJM Evidence*, 2022. — 22 UK NHS hospitals, 538 patients, PDD-TURBT vs. WL-TURBT, primary endpoint time to recurrence at 3 years. Photodynamic diagnosis (PDD) provides better diagnostic accuracy and more complete tumor resection and may reduce recurrence, but there is limited evidence on longer-term clinical effectiveness and cost-effectiveness. PDD-guided TURBT did not reduce recurrence rates, nor was it cost-effective compared with WL at 3 years.
 - Companion protocol/HTA report: Heer R, et al. "Photodynamic versus white-light-guided resection of first-diagnosis non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer: PHOTO RCT." *Health Technology Assessment*, 2022;26(40).
 - Cost-effectiveness companion paper: Yu G, et al. *European Urology Open Science*, 2023.

2. **Geavlete B, Multescu R, Georgescu D, et al.** "HAL blue-light cystoscopy in high-risk nonmuscle-invasive bladder cancer—re-TURBT recurrence rates in a prospective, randomized study." *Urology*, 2010;76(3):664-9. — 446 patients randomized. The overall short-term recurrence rate at Re-TURBT was 11.1% for the blue-light group and 31.2% for the white-light group, with blue-light cystoscopy significantly reducing short-term recurrence rates across all high-risk categories.

3. **O'Brien T, Ray E, Chatterton K, Khan MS, Chandra A, Thomas K.** "Prospective randomized trial of hexylaminolevulinate photodynamic-assisted TURBT plus single-shot intravesical mitomycin C vs conventional white-light TURBT plus mitomycin C in newly presenting NMIBC." *BJU International*, 2013;112(8):1096-1104. — 249 patients, Guy's Hospital.

4. **Schumacher MC, Holmäng S, Davidsson T, Friedrich B, Pedersen J, Wiklund NP.** "Transurethral resection of non-muscle-invasive bladder transitional cell cancers with or without 5-aminolevulinic acid under visible and fluorescent light: results of a prospective, randomised, multicentre study." *European Urology*, 2010;57(2):293-9.

5. **Stenzl A, Burger M, Fradet Y, et al.** "Hexaminolevulinate guided fluorescence cystoscopy reduces recurrence in patients with nonmuscle invasive bladder cancer." *Journal of Urology*, 2010;184(5):1907-14. — Pooled analysis of prospective randomized trials.

6. **Grossman HB, Gomella L, Fradet Y, et al.** "A phase III multicenter comparison of hexaminolevulinate fluorescence cystoscopy and white light cystoscopy for the detection of superficial papillary lesions in patients with bladder cancer." *Journal of Urology*, 2007;178:62-67.

7. Randomized trial reporting negative long-term result: **"Hexaminolevulinate-guided transurethral resection of non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer does not reduce the recurrence rates after a 2-year follow up: a prospective randomized trial."** *International Urology and Nephrology*, 2014;46:927-33.

**Other primary comparative/RCT designs**

8. **"Efficacy and safety of a novel 450 nm blue diode laser versus plasmakinetic electrocautery for the transurethral resection of non-muscle invasive bladder cancer."** Multicenter RCT (protocol + results), PMC9887014. — The novel blue laser could be effectively and safely used for TURBt in patients with NMIBC, was non-inferior to plasmakinetic electrocautery during the perioperative period, and may reduce blood loss and accelerate wound healing, though longer follow-up was needed to confirm recurrence-free survival benefit. (Note: this trial is about a blue diode *laser* resection tool, not blue-light/photodynamic fluorescence-guided cystoscopy — worth flagging as it may not fit your PICO exactly.)

9. **HELENA study**: Prospective randomized controlled open-label multicenter non-inferiority study, 129 patients (7 European centers, 2010–2016), comparing Hexvix-TURB followed by immediate instillation versus white-light TURB with immediate instillation followed by maintenance intravesical chemotherapy, with recurrence-free survival at 12 months as the primary endpoint.

10. Real-world/prospective controlled (non-randomized but individual primary study) comparison: **"'Real-life experience': recurrence rate at 3 years with Hexvix photodynamic diagnosis-assisted TURBT compared with good quality white light TURBT in new NMIBC—a prospective controlled study."** *World Journal of Urology*, 2017. (Note: not randomized — controlled/matched prospective cohort; flag if you need RCT-only.)

11. **Chan et al.** "Blue light transurethral resection and biopsy of bladder cancer with hexaminolevulinate: Histopathological characteristics and recurrence rates in a single UK centre study." *BJUI Compass*, 2023. (Note: non-randomized single-center cohort — also flag if RCT-only is required.)
