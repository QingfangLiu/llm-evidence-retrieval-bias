## Summary of findings

**Adult hospitalized patients (non-obstetric, non-hematologic):**
- No dedicated RCT was found on single- vs multi-unit transfusion in general medical/surgical inpatients broadly. Most direct evidence comes from **hematology/oncology** and **obstetric** populations.

**Hematology/oncology inpatients:**
- The **1versus2 trial** (Chantepie et al., *Blood* 2021 abstract; protocol in *Trials* 2015) — a multicenter RCT of 245 patients undergoing chemotherapy or stem cell transplant — found single-unit transfusion **non-inferior** to double-unit transfusion for a composite safety endpoint, with similar total RBC units used per patient overall.
- A separate multicenter RCT (*Bone Marrow Transplant/ScienceDirect* 2023) in similar patients found single-unit policy lowered discharge hemoglobin and total units used slightly, with no difference in length of stay, bleeding, or mortality.

**Obstetrics (postpartum anemia):**
- Hamm et al. (*Am J Obstet Gynecol* 2021, SMaRT Blood trial) — RCT of 66 women — single-unit protocol led to fewer total units transfused, lower post-transfusion hemoglobin, but no differences in symptoms, length of stay, or 30-day complications.

**Pediatrics/neonates:**
- Paul et al. (*J Pediatr Hematol Oncol* 2002) — RCT of 10 vs 20 mL/kg in very-low-birth-weight infants — 20 mL/kg produced greater hemoglobin/hematocrit rise with no pulmonary function difference.
- Wang et al. (*Ther Clin Risk Manag* 2023) — RCT of an individualized, score-guided transfusion strategy vs standard care in children undergoing major non-cardiac surgery — reduced blood exposure without increased complications.
- Kaur et al. (*Transfusion* 2022) — RCT in thalassemia children comparing weight-based vs formula-based dosing — formula-based dosing reduced donor exposures and annual blood requirement.

A closely related but distinct body of evidence (restrictive vs. liberal **hemoglobin-trigger thresholds**, e.g., TRICC, FOCUS, SAHaRA) addresses *when* to transfuse rather than *how much per episode*, so those weren't included unless you'd like them added.
