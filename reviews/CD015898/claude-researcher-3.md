## Adult and general hospitalized populations

- **Atilla E, et al. (2017)** — Randomized comparison of hemoglobin content-based vs. standard unit-based RBC transfusion policy. *Turkish Journal of Hematology*.
- **French Ministry of Health–funded trial (2023)** — Multicenter RCT of single-unit vs. double-unit RBC transfusion in hematological malignancy/HSCT patients ("1versus2" trial). *ScienceDirect/Transfusion Clinique et Biologique* (protocol also published in *Trials*, 2015).
- **Webert KE, et al. (2008)** — Pilot RCT of standard (transfuse at Hb <80 g/L) vs. augmented (transfuse at Hb <120 g/L) 2-unit transfusion strategy in acute leukemia/HSCT patients. *Transfusion*.
- **Prick BW, et al. (2014)** — Single-unit vs. multiple-unit (SMaRT Blood–type) transfusion protocol trial in hemodynamically stable postpartum anemia. *American Journal of Obstetrics & Gynecology*.
- **Prakash D, et al.** — Postoperative RBC transfusion strategy trial in frail anemic elderly hip-fracture patients reporting units per episode (TRIFE) — note this is primarily a threshold (liberal vs. restrictive) trial, but it reports per-episode unit dosing outcomes. *Acta Orthopaedica* / *European Journal of Orthopaedic Surgery & Traumatology*.

## Pediatric populations

- **Maitland K, et al. (2019)** — Immediate transfusion in African children with uncomplicated severe anemia: factorial RCT of 20 mL/kg vs. 30 mL/kg whole-blood-equivalent. *New England Journal of Medicine* (TRACT trial, immediate-transfusion component).
- **Maitland K, et al. (2019)** — Transfusion volume for children with severe anemia in Africa: RCT of 20 mL/kg vs. 30 mL/kg. *New England Journal of Medicine* (TRACT trial, volume component).
- **Kiguli S, et al. (2014)** — Phase II trial of standard (20 mL/kg) vs. increased (30 mL/kg) transfusion volume in Ugandan children with acute severe anemia. *BMC Medicine*.

## Preterm/neonatal populations

- **Wong ECC, Perez-Albuerne E, Moscoso P, Luban NLC (2005)** — RCT of transfusion volume in very-low-birth-weight infants: 10 mL/kg vs. 20 mL/kg. *Journal of Pediatric Hematology/Oncology*.
- **Balegar VKK, Kluckow M (2011)** — RCT of furosemide use with packed RBC transfusion in preterm infants (relevant to volume/fluid load effects). *Journal of Pediatrics*.
- **Choi YB, et al. (2021)** — Hemodynamic effects of different transfusion volumes in preterm infants (comparative/randomized design). *Pediatrics International*.
- **Author(s), study from North India (CTRI/2018/01/011063)** — RCT comparing weight-based vs. formula-based (hematocrit-adjusted) PRBC transfusion volume calculation in preterm neonates. *PMC/journal not fully specified in search*.
- **Bell EF, et al. (2005)** — Randomized trial of liberal vs. restrictive guidelines for RBC transfusion in preterm infants (Iowa trial) — primarily threshold-based but reports transfused volumes; often cross-referenced in volume-per-transfusion reviews.
- **Franz AR, et al. / PINT investigators, Kirpalani H, et al. (2006)** — Premature Infants in Need of Transfusion (PINT) trial — threshold trial with volume data, frequently cited alongside dose trials.

- **PlaNeT-3** (NCT06095648) — RCT of platelet transfusion *volumes* in preterm neonates (not RBC, but methodologically parallel; may be useful context).
