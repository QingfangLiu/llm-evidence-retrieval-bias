I have enough to compile a solid, categorized list of primary studies (RCTs and controlled trials only — no reviews/meta-analyses/guidelines). Here's what I found across the major advanced sperm selection technique categories:

## Hyaluronan-based selection (PICSI / HA-ICSI)

- **Miller et al., HABSelect trial** — Lancet, 2019. Parallel, two-group RCT, 2,772 couples. Live birth rate did not differ significantly between PICSI (27.4%) and ICSI (25.2%) groups (OR 1.12).
- **Alegre et al.** — Biomedicines, 2025. Triple-blind RCT in oocyte-donation couples. Pregnancy rate was higher in the PICSI group than ICSI (74.04% vs. 70.87%), and cumulative pregnancy rate was significantly higher in the PICSI group (88% vs. lower control rate).
- **Korosi et al.** — Eur Rev Med Pharmacol Sci, 2017. RCT of PICSI outcomes with oral myo-inositol pretreatment in oligoasthenoteratozoospermic men.
- **Novoselsky Persky et al.** — Andrology, 2021. Sibling-oocyte trial comparing conventional ICSI vs. PICSI.

## IMSI (ultra-high magnification morphological selection)

- **Leandri et al.** — Andrology, 2013. Multicentric RCT, 255 couples, first ART attempt. IMSI did not provide significant improvement in implantation (24% vs 23%), clinical pregnancy (31% vs 33%), or live birth rates (27% vs 30%).
- **Antinori et al.** — earlier RCT in severe male factor infertility; IMSI resulted in significantly higher implantation (17.3% vs 11.3%) and clinical pregnancy rates (39.2% vs 26.5%) per the Leandri paper's summary of it (locate original for full citation).
- Note: one IMSI RCT (Reprod Biol Endocrinol, 2015) was later **retracted** — worth flagging/excluding from synthesis: "Intracytoplasmic morphologically selected sperm injection versus conventional intracytoplasmic sperm injection: a randomized controlled trial" — RETRACTED ARTICLE.

## Magnetic-activated cell sorting (MACS, Annexin-V)

- **Romany et al.** — J Assist Reprod Genet, 2017. RCT (referenced within multiple sources), 237 couples, ovum donation. Applying MACS to remove annexin V-positive sperm cells from unselected males does not improve reproductive outcome of ICSI in ovum donation.
- **Prospective randomized trial (Iranian group)** — 62 semen samples, MACS-DGC vs. DGC alone. No significant difference in fertilization rates, but percentage of high-quality embryos, pregnancy, and implantation rates were significantly higher in the MACS-DGC group.
- **Effect of MACS in D-IUI RCT** — multicenter, 181 cycles. No significant differences in gestation, live birth, or miscarriage rates between MACS and control groups.

## Zeta potential selection

- **Zeta RCT (Royan Institute)** — double-blind, 203 ICSI cycles. Zeta procedure improves pregnancy outcome and alters sex ratio of developed pregnancies.
- **DGC/Zeta RCT** — single-blind, 220 couples with male factor infertility, re-evaluating efficiency of DGC/Zeta vs. DGC alone.

## Microfluidic sperm sorting (MFSS)

- **Yetkinel et al.** — J Assist Reprod Genet, 2019. Prospective RCT, unexplained infertility, microfluidic chip vs. standard selection.
- **NCT03085433** — "Sperm Selection by Microfluidic Separation Improves Embryo Quality," registered RCT, 386 randomized participants, ClinicalTrials.gov.
- **Ozcan et al.** — J Obstet Gynaecol Res, 2021. Microfluidic sorting in male factor infertility.
- **Ferrer Buitrago et al.** — prospective non-inferiority sibling-oocyte pilot comparing in-situ microfluidics vs. DGC (Hum Reprod, 2023 conference abstract).
- **Comparative Evaluation of Two Microfluidic Sperm Sorting Devices** — 2026, prospective comparison, ZyMōt vs. DGC vs. CA0.

## Cumulus-cell column selection

- **Sperm Selection Using Cumulus Cell Column...RCT** — recent RCT, 88 ICSI cycles. Higher implantation (58% vs. 28.4%), clinical pregnancy (77.3% vs. 25%), and live birth rates (72.7% vs. 25%) in the cumulus-cell-column group vs. controls.
