## Summary

Below are individual **primary studies** (trials/pilot trials — not reviews, meta-analyses, guidelines, or editorials) that evaluate smoking-cessation interventions **initiated on a psychiatric inpatient ward** in adults being treated for mental illness. All were identified via PubMed/journal search.

**Key findings pattern across trials:** interventions typically combine brief motivational counselling started on the ward, NRT (often started during the smoke-free admission), and structured post-discharge follow-up (telephone counselling, computer/app-based sustained care, or extended pharmacotherapy). Several trials (Prochaska 2014, Metse/Wye 2017, Stockings 2014, Brown/Helping HAND 3 2021) found higher biochemically-verified abstinence in the intervention arm at 3–6 months post-discharge, with effects attenuating by 12–18 months in some studies. One trial (Prochaska 2014) also found lower psychiatric rehospitalization in the intervention group.
