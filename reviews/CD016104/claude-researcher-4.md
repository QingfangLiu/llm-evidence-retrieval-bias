## Summary of the evidence landscape

**Neoadjuvant-only:**
- CheckMate 816 randomly assigned patients with stage IB to IIIA resectable NSCLC to receive nivolumab plus platinum-based chemotherapy or platinum-based chemotherapy alone before resection, and found that this neoadjuvant nivolumab plus chemotherapy had a significant benefit over chemotherapy alone with respect to event-free survival and pathological complete response without impeding surgical feasibility.

**Perioperative (neoadjuvant + adjuvant) regimens:**
- KEYNOTE-671 showed that adding pembrolizumab to neoadjuvant cisplatin-based chemotherapy, followed by surgical resection and adjuvant pembrolizumab, significantly improved event-free survival, major pathological response, and pathological complete response in stage II–III disease.
- AEGEAN found that perioperative durvalumab plus neoadjuvant chemotherapy was associated with significantly greater event-free survival and pathological complete response than neoadjuvant chemotherapy alone, with a safety profile consistent with the individual agents.
- CheckMate 77T found that perioperative nivolumab resulted in significantly longer event-free survival than perioperative placebo added to neoadjuvant chemotherapy in resectable NSCLC.
- The Neotorch trial compared toripalimab in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy versus chemotherapy alone on event-free survival and major pathological response in patients with stage III resectable NSCLC, demonstrating significant EFS improvement.
- NADIM II, a Spanish open-label phase 2 trial, randomly assigned patients with resectable stage III NSCLC to neoadjuvant nivolumab plus platinum-based chemotherapy versus chemotherapy alone, followed by surgery, with adjuvant nivolumab for patients with R0 resections, showing improved pathological complete response and survival.

**Adjuvant-only (after surgery ± chemotherapy):**
- IMpower010 randomly assigned patients with completely resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC to receive adjuvant atezolizumab or best supportive care after adjuvant platinum-based chemotherapy, showing improved disease-free survival, particularly in PD-L1–expressing tumors.
- PEARLS/KEYNOTE-091 randomly assigned patients with completely resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC to pembrolizumab or placebo, showing significantly improved disease-free survival irrespective of PD-L1 expression status.

**A note on your "older adults" focus:** none of these pivotal trials were designed specifically for older adults — older adults and women are underrepresented in neoadjuvant and perioperative clinical trials with immune checkpoint blockers — but several report prespecified or exploratory age-based (typically ≥65 or ≥75 years) subgroup analyses of efficacy and toxicity, which you'll want to extract directly from each primary paper (or its supplementary appendix) rather than relying on secondary sources.
