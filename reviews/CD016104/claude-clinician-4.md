## Overview

For resectable, early-stage/locally-advanced (limited to one area, without distant metastases) non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), several large randomized controlled trials (RCTs) have tested immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) in the neoadjuvant, adjuvant, or perioperative (neoadjuvant + adjuvant) setting, alone or combined with platinum-based chemotherapy, versus placebo or chemotherapy alone. Below is a summary organized by treatment strategy, followed by the primary-study reference list you requested.

**Neoadjuvant only (ICI + chemo before surgery, no post-op ICI continuation as randomized variable):**
CheckMate 816 established that nivolumab plus chemotherapy had a significant benefit over chemotherapy alone with respect to event-free survival and pathological complete response, with no adverse effect on surgical feasibility or surgical outcomes. A subsequent update showed a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in overall survival with the addition of nivolumab to platinum-doublet chemotherapy as neoadjuvant treatment.

**Adjuvant only (ICI after surgery ± chemo, vs. placebo/best supportive care):**
- IMpower010: this randomised, multicentre, open-label, phase 3 study evaluated adjuvant atezolizumab versus best supportive care after adjuvant platinum-based chemotherapy in resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC, showing a disease-free survival benefit with atezolizumab versus best supportive care, with pronounced benefit in the subgroup whose tumours expressed PD-L1 on 1% or more of tumour cells. At ≥5-year follow-up, benefit persisted in patients with resected stage II-IIIA PD-L1–selected NSCLC.
- KEYNOTE-091 (PEARLS): participants were randomly assigned to pembrolizumab 200 mg or placebo, both administered intravenously every 3 weeks for up to 18 cycles, in a triple-blind design. Pembrolizumab led to a significant DFS improvement compared with placebo in the intention-to-treat population (HR, 0.76; 95% CI, 0.63-0.91; P = .0014).

**Perioperative (neoadjuvant + adjuvant ICI, vs. neoadjuvant chemo alone/placebo):**
- CheckMate 77T: patients received neoadjuvant nivolumab at 360 mg plus platinum-doublet chemotherapy or neoadjuvant chemotherapy plus placebo, followed by surgery and adjuvant nivolumab or placebo, with a statistically significant and clinically meaningful event-free survival benefit and improved pathologic complete response rate vs placebo plus chemotherapy.
- KEYNOTE-671: participants with resectable stage II, IIIA, or IIIB (N2 stage) NSCLC were assigned to receive neoadjuvant pembrolizumab or placebo with cisplatin-based chemotherapy for 4 cycles, followed by surgery and adjuvant pembrolizumab or placebo for up to 13 cycles.
- AEGEAN: this phase 3, international, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial investigated durvalumab administered perioperatively along with neoadjuvant chemotherapy, showing significantly higher rates of pathologic complete response (17.2% vs 4.3%) and significantly longer event-free survival (HR 0.68; 95% CI 0.53–0.88) compared with neoadjuvant chemotherapy alone.
- Neotorch: this randomized clinical trial compared toripalimab in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy vs chemotherapy alone on event-free survival and major pathological response in patients with stage III resectable NSCLC.
- NADIM II (phase II, smaller but randomized): patients with resectable clinical stage IIIA NSCLC were randomized to nivolumab plus paclitaxel/carboplatin followed by surgery and adjuvant nivolumab, versus chemotherapy alone followed by surgery, with overall survival of 85.3% at 24 months with neoadjuvant nivolumab plus chemotherapy vs 64.8% with neoadjuvant chemotherapy alone, and pathologic complete response of 36.2% vs 6.8%.

**Note on your specific population (older adults):** none of these trials were designed specifically for elderly patients, but all report pre-specified age-based subgroup analyses (commonly <65 vs. ≥65, sometimes ≥75) that you can pull from each primary paper's forest plots/supplementary tables.
