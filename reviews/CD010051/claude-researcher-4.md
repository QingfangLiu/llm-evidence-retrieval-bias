## Summary

The studies below are individual primary trials (RCTs, dose-ranging trials, open-label extensions, and one retrospective cohort/claims study), covering the major topical cyclosporine A formulations used for dry eye disease (DED): Restasis (0.05%/0.1% emulsion), Ikervis/Cyclokat (0.1% cationic emulsion), Cequa/OTX-101 (0.09% nanomicellar), CyclASol/Vevye (water-free 0.1%), and generic/novel formulations. They report on efficacy signs (Schirmer test, corneal/conjunctival staining, TBUT), symptoms (OSDI, SANDE, VAS), and harms (adverse events, burning/stinging, discontinuation).

**Note:** I excluded systematic reviews, meta-analyses, narrative reviews, and guidelines from the list — several turned up in search (e.g., a Sjögren's syndrome DED systematic review, a CsA 0.05% meta-analysis, a seven-formulation network meta-analysis, and narrative reviews) and are not included below.
