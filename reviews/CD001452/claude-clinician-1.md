Based on my searches, here are the individual primary studies (RCTs) comparing venipuncture to heel lance for blood sampling in full-term newborns. All are excluded of reviews/meta-analyses/guidelines per your request.

**Summary of findings**

Across these trials, venipuncture is consistently associated with less pain (lower Neonatal Facial Coding System or Neonatal Infant Pain Scale scores, shorter crying duration) and greater efficiency (fewer repeat punctures/less need for re-sampling) than heel lance in healthy, full-term infants — provided it is performed by a skilled, trained phlebotomist. One trial found combining venipuncture with oral sucrose did not add further benefit over venipuncture alone.

**Primary studies (individual trials):**

1. **Larsson BA, Tannfeldt G, Lagercrantz H, Olsson GL.** Venipuncture is more effective and less painful than heel lancing for blood tests in neonates. *Pediatrics.* 1998 May;101(5):882–886. — RCT, n=120 full-term infants (venipuncture vs. small lancet vs. large lancet heel lance).

2. **Shah VS, Taddio A, Bennett S, Speidel BD.** Neonatal pain response to heel stick vs venepuncture for routine blood sampling. *Arch Dis Child Fetal Neonatal Ed.* 1997 Sep;77(2):F143–F144. — RCT, n=27 infants.

3. **Ogawa S, Ogihara T, Fujiwara E, Ito K, Nakano M, Nakayama S, Hachiya T, Fujimoto N, Abe H, Ban S, Ikeda E, Tamai H.** Venepuncture is preferable to heel lance for blood sampling in term neonates. *Arch Dis Child Fetal Neonatal Ed.* 2005 Sep;90(5):F432–F436. — RCT, n=100 healthy full-term infants, 2×2 design (venipuncture vs. heel lance, with or without sucrose).

4. **Larsson BA, Tannfeldt G, Lagercrantz H, Olsson GL.** Alleviation of the pain of venepuncture in neonates. *Acta Paediatr.* 1998 Jul;87(7):774–779. — RCT, n=120 neonates (EMLA vs. placebo before venipuncture); a companion study to #1, relevant to venipuncture technique/pain in the same population.

5. **Logan PW.** Venepuncture versus heel prick for the collection of the newborn screening test. *Aust J Adv Nurs.* 1999;17(2):30–36. — Comparative/trial study of newborn screening blood collection methods.
