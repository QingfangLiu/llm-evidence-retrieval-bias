**Faster aspart (Fiasp) vs. conventional insulin aspart (MDI, adults with T1D)**
- **ONSET 1** (Russell-Jones et al., *Diabetes Care* 2017): 26-week, treat-to-target RCT with insulin detemir as basal. Faster aspart effectively improved HbA1c, with noninferiority to insulin aspart confirmed, and superior postprandial glucose control for mealtime faster aspart versus insulin aspart.
- **Mathieu et al., 52-week extension of ONSET 1** (*Diabetes Obes Metab* 2018) — longer-term follow-up of the same trial population.
- **ONSET 8** (Buse et al., *Diabetes Obes Metab* 2018): individuals with T1D were randomized to receive faster aspart or insulin aspart, both in combination with insulin degludec; noninferiority to mealtime insulin aspart was confirmed, and mealtime faster aspart demonstrated superiority in the 1-hour postprandial glucose increment.

**Ultra-rapid lispro (Lyumjev/URLi) vs. insulin lispro (MDI, adults with T1D)**
- **PRONTO-T1D** (Klaff et al., *Diabetes Obes Metab* 2020): 26-week, treat-to-target phase 3 trial; both mealtime and postmeal URLi demonstrated noninferiority to lispro for HbA1c change, with a significantly higher endpoint HbA1c for postmeal URLi versus lispro.
- **PRONTO-T1D CGM substudy** (Malecki et al.) — time-in-range outcomes from the same trial.
- **PRONTO-T1D Extension** (*Diabetes Therapy* 2021): long-term efficacy and safety follow-up comparing URLi and lispro as part of an MDI regimen in adults with T1D.

**Insulin lispro vs. regular human insulin (MDI, adults with T1D)**
- **UK Trial Group** (Brunelle et al./various, *Diabet Med* 2000): prospective, randomized, double-blind, crossover comparison of insulin lispro with human soluble insulin in patients on multiple daily doses, with a 6-week run-in and 12 weeks on each therapy (93 patients).

**Insulin aspart vs. regular human insulin (MDI, adults with T1D)**
- **Home, Lindholm, Riis (European Insulin Aspart Study Group)**, *Diabet Med* 2000 — insulin aspart vs. human insulin, long-term blood glucose control RCT.
- **Raskin et al.**, *Diabetes Care* 2000 — insulin aspart as mealtime insulin vs. regular human insulin.
- **Tamás et al.**, *Diabetes Res Clin Pract* 2001 — multinational RCT of optimized insulin aspart vs. human insulin.
- **DeVries, Lindholm, Jacobsen, Heine, Home (Tri-Continental Insulin Aspart Study Group)**, *Diabet Med* 2003: randomized trial comparing insulin aspart with intensified basal NPH insulin supplementation versus human regular insulin with standard basal NPH insulin in people with type 1 diabetes, 43 centres in seven countries.
- **Heller et al.**, *Diabet Med* 2004 — double-blind, randomized, crossover trial of hypoglycaemia with insulin aspart vs. regular insulin.
- **Home et al.**, *Diabetes Res Clin Pract* 2006 — pre-meal insulin aspart vs. pre-meal soluble human insulin.
- **Hermansen et al.**, *Diabetologia* 2004: 18-week, randomized, open-label, parallel trial in 595 patients with type 1 diabetes comparing insulin detemir + insulin aspart versus NPH insulin + regular human insulin in basal-bolus therapy; glycaemic control was improved with the analogue combination.

**Insulin glulisine vs. regular human insulin (MDI, adults with T1D)**
- **Garg, Rosenstock, Ways**, *Endocr Pract* 2005: open-label, randomized, controlled, multicenter, parallel-group, 12-week study in 860 patients with type 1 diabetes on insulin glargine, comparing premeal or postmeal insulin glulisine with premeal regular human insulin.
