## Overview

I searched for individual primary studies (randomized controlled trials) comparing faster-acting insulin analogues with regular human insulin, or with another faster-acting analogue, in adults with type 1 diabetes on multiple daily injections (MDI). I excluded systematic reviews, meta-analyses, narrative reviews, guidelines, and editorials — though a few of those surfaced in search results and are noted only as sources I did *not* include.

Below is an organized summary by comparison type, followed by the full reference list.

### 1. Faster aspart vs. conventional insulin aspart (adults, T1D, MDI)
- **ONSET 1** (Russell-Jones et al., *Diabetes Care* 2017) — 26-week RCT, faster aspart vs. IAsp with insulin detemir; HbA1c was reduced in both treatment groups, with noninferiority confirmed for both mealtime and postmeal faster aspart, and mealtime faster aspart statistically significantly reduced HbA1c versus IAsp, with statistically significantly lower postprandial glucose increments at 1 and 2 hours for mealtime faster aspart.
- **ONSET 1 52-week extension** (Mathieu et al., *Diabetes Obes Metab* 2018)
- **ONSET 8** (Buse et al., *Diabetes Obes Metab* 2018) — faster aspart vs. IAsp with insulin degludec; noninferiority to mealtime insulin aspart was confirmed for mealtime or post-meal faster aspart for HbA1c, with superiority for mealtime faster aspart in 1-h postprandial glucose increment.
- **Post hoc analysis of ONSET 1 and ONSET 8** by carbohydrate-counting status (Klonoff et al., *PMC*)

### 2. Faster aspart vs. IAsp in non-MDI/related delivery contexts (adults, T1D) — included as they inform the broader analogue comparison
- Hybrid closed-loop crossover trial (Lee et al., *Diabetes Care* 2021)
- Insulin pump (non-automated) crossover RCT (Ranjan et al., *Diabetes Obes Metab* 2025)

### 3. Ultra-rapid lispro (URLi) vs. insulin lispro (adults, T1D, MDI)
- **PRONTO-T1D** (Klaff et al., *Diabetes Obes Metab* 2020) — 26-week RCT; both mealtime and postmeal URLi demonstrated non-inferiority to lispro for HbA1c, and mealtime URLi improved postprandial glucose control.
- PRONTO-T1D 52-week long-term extension (Diabetes Ther, 2020)
- PRONTO-T1D CGM substudy (Bergenstal et al., *PMC*/PubMed 2020)
- PRONTO-T1D Japanese subpopulation analyses (26-week and 52-week)

### 4. Rapid-acting analogues vs. regular human insulin (adults, T1D, MDI) — classic head-to-head trials
- **Anderson et al., *Diabetes* 1997** — large multinational lispro vs. regular human insulin trial; a 6-month randomized multinational (17 countries) and multicenter (102 investigators) clinical trial with an open-label crossover design comparing insulin lispro and regular human insulin in mealtime treatment of 1,008 patients with IDDM.
- Torlone et al. (1996) — crossover RCT of lispro vs. regular human insulin
- Home et al., *Diabet Med* 2000 — insulin aspart vs. human insulin, long-term
- Raskin et al., *Diabetes Care* 2000 — insulin aspart as mealtime insulin vs. human insulin
- **Tamás et al., *Diabetes Res Clin Pract* 2001** — prospective, multicentre, randomised, open-label study with parallel groups in 48 centres in 11 countries, 423 basal-bolus treated patients with Type 1 diabetes, comparing insulin aspart with human insulin after algorithm-driven dose optimisation.
- **Home et al., *Diabetes Res Clin Pract* 2006** — 30-month extension; insulin aspart was significantly superior to human insulin for overall glycaemic control, with similar major hypoglycaemia risk but higher minor hypoglycaemia risk.
- Optimized basal-bolus regimens: **glulisine vs. regular human insulin** (Dailey et al., *Endocrine Practice*) — an open-label, randomized, controlled, multicenter, parallel-group, 12-week study in 860 patients with type 1 diabetes comparing premeal or postmeal glulisine with premeal regular human insulin, each with insulin glargine, with greater A1c reduction for premeal glulisine versus regular human insulin.

### 5. Head-to-head comparisons between rapid-acting analogues (adults, T1D, MDI)
- Glargine + lispro vs. NPH + human insulin (Ashwell et al., *Diabet Med* 2006)
- Insulin glulisine vs. insulin lispro (adult, CSII-based — Van Bon et al., included for completeness though pump-based)

### 6. Pregnancy-related (T1D, MDI-eligible adults)
- **CopenFast** (Nørgaard et al., *Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol* 2023) — faster aspart vs. insulin aspart in pregnancy/post-delivery, T1D and T2D, MDI or pump.
