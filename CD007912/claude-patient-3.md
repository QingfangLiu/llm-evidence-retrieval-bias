Based on these searches, I found individual primary studies (RCTs) that directly investigate land-based exercise for hip osteoarthritis. Here's a summary of what the evidence shows before the list:

Across these trials, land-based therapeutic exercise (strengthening, patient education plus supervised exercise, physiotherapist-led programs, or manual therapy plus exercise) generally produces small-to-moderate short-term improvements in hip pain, with more mixed results for physical function. Effects tend to be modest, sometimes not statistically significant against a sham/placebo comparator, and long-term effects on structural outcomes like need for hip replacement are less consistent — one trial found exercise+education postponed total hip replacement over ~6 years, but a related earlier analysis found no significant self-reported pain difference at 16 months.

## Primary studies (individual trials, not reviews)

1. **Bennell KL, Egerton T, Martin J, et al.** Effect of physical therapy on pain and function in patients with hip osteoarthritis: a randomized clinical trial. *JAMA*. 2014;311(19):1987-97.

2. **Krauss I, Steinhilber B, Haupt G, et al.** Exercise therapy in hip osteoarthritis—a randomized controlled trial (THüKo trial). *Dtsch Arztebl Int*. 2014;111(35-36):592-9.

3. **Fernandes L, Storheim K, Sandvik L, et al.** Efficacy of patient education and supervised exercise vs patient education alone in patients with hip osteoarthritis: a single blind randomized clinical trial. *Osteoarthritis Cartilage*. 2010;18(10):1237-43.

4. **Svege I, Nordsletten L, Fernandes L, Risberg MA.** Exercise therapy may postpone total hip replacement surgery in patients with hip osteoarthritis: a long-term follow-up of a randomized trial. *Ann Rheum Dis*. 2015;74(1):164-9.

5. **French HP, Cusack T, Brennan A, et al.** Exercise and Manual Physiotherapy Arthritis Research Trial (EMPART) for osteoarthritis of the hip: a multicenter randomized controlled trial. *Arch Phys Med Rehabil*. 2013;94(2):302-14.

6. **Bennell KL, Nelligan RK, Rini C, et al.** Effects of internet-based pain coping skills training before home exercise for individuals with hip osteoarthritis (HOPE trial): a randomised controlled trial. *Pain*. 2018;159(9):1833-42.

7. **van der Esch et al./van Bodegom-Vos or similar Dutch pragmatic trial** — reported as: Effectiveness of exercise therapy added to general practitioner care in patients with hip osteoarthritis: a pragmatic randomized controlled trial. *Osteoarthritis Cartilage*. 2015.

8. **Krauss et al. / follow-up analysis:** Secondary analysis of a study on exercise therapy in hip osteoarthritis: follow-up data on pain and physical functioning. *J Clin Med* (MDPI). 2021.

9. **(Pilot RCT)** Medical exercise therapy in patients with hip osteoarthritis: a randomized controlled trial with six months follow-up — a pilot study. *Musculoskelet Sci Pract* (ScienceDirect). 2016.
