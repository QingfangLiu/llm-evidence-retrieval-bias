I found primary studies (RCTs, not reviews/meta-analyses) evaluating weight-loss medications and health outcomes in people with hypertension or elevated blood pressure. These span several drug classes — GLP-1/GIP receptor agonists, serotonergic appetite suppressants, lipase inhibitors, and combination agents — and report either cardiovascular clinical outcomes or blood-pressure-specific endpoints in hypertensive or high-BP subgroups.

**GLP-1/GIP receptor agonists**
- Lincoff AM, Brown-Frandsen K, Colhoun HM, et al. Semaglutide and cardiovascular outcomes in obesity without diabetes (SELECT trial). *N Engl J Med.* 2023;389(24):2221-2232. — 17,604 patients with established CVD and overweight/obesity (82% had a history of hypertension); MACE reduced 20% with semaglutide vs placebo.
- Jastreboff AM, Aronne LJ, Ahmad NN, et al. Tirzepatide Once Weekly for the Treatment of Obesity (SURMOUNT-1 trial). *N Engl J Med.* 2022;387:205-216. — includes blood pressure as a secondary endpoint in participants with weight-related comorbidities including hypertension.
- Kosiborod MN, et al. Tirzepatide in Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction and Obesity (SUMMIT trial). *N Engl J Med.* 2024. — randomized trial in HFpEF/obesity showing tirzepatide reduced systolic blood pressure and improved outcomes.

**Serotonin 2C receptor agonist**
- Bohula EA, Wiviott SD, McGuire DK, et al. Cardiovascular Safety of Lorcaserin in Overweight or Obese Patients (CAMELLIA-TIMI 61 trial). *N Engl J Med.* 2018;379(12):1107-1117. — 12,000 overweight/obese patients (90% with baseline hypertension) randomized to lorcaserin vs placebo; reported effects on blood pressure and CV events.

**Naltrexone-bupropion**
- Nissen SE, Wolski KE, Prcela L, et al. Effect of naltrexone-bupropion on major adverse cardiovascular events in overweight and obese patients with cardiovascular risk factors: a randomized clinical trial. *JAMA.* 2016;315(10):990-1004.

**Phentermine-topiramate**
- Gadde KM, Allison DB, Ryan DH, et al. Effects of low-dose, controlled-release, phentermine plus topiramate combination on weight and associated comorbidities in overweight and obese adults (CONQUER trial). *Lancet.* 2011;377(9774):1341-1352.

**Orlistat (lipase inhibitor)**
- Torgerson JS, Hauptman J, Boldrin MN, Sjöström L. XENical in the Prevention of Diabetes in Obese Subjects (XENDOS) study. *Diabetes Care.* 2004;27(1):155-161. — 4-year RCT; reported effects on blood pressure and CV risk factors alongside diabetes prevention.
- Bakris G, Calhoun D, Egan B, et al. Orlistat improves blood pressure control in obese subjects with treated but inadequately controlled hypertension. *J Hypertens.* 2002;20(11):2257-2267. — RCT specifically in obese, treated hypertensive patients.
- Sharma AM, Golay A. Orlistat in hypertensive overweight/obese patients: results of a randomized clinical trial. *J Hypertens.* 2003;21(11):2159-2165.
