**Diabetes**
- Greenwood et al. 2015, *JMIR* — 2-arm RCT of telehealth remote monitoring with paired glucose testing vs. usual care in type 2 diabetes (6-month A1c outcome)
- (Nigeria) — 2-arm RCT of pharmacist-led telephone/SMS follow-up vs. usual care in type 2 diabetes, tertiary hospital diabetes clinic, 309 patients, 25 weeks (adherence, glycemic control, knowledge)

- FRIEND trial — RCT of flash glucose monitoring + remote intervention vs. FGM alone in type 1 diabetes (12-week HbA1c)
- Evaluation of remote pharmacist intervention on HbA1c in an accountable care organization — retrospective multicenter cohort study

**Hypertension**
- Hyperlink 3 trial (Margolis et al.), *Hypertension* — pragmatic cluster-RCT comparing pharmacist-led telehealth care vs. clinic-based care for uncontrolled BP
- Ibrahim et al. 2022 — community-setting telepharmacy study on BP control, adherence, and drug-related problem detection
- Pharmacist-led telemedicine hypertension management pilot study during COVID-19 (*Frontiers in Public Health*)

**Multi-condition (hyperlipidemia/hypertension/diabetes)**
- STIC2IT trial (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02512276) — cluster-RCT of tele-pharmacist intervention for medication adherence/disease control across hyperlipidemia, hypertension, and diabetes

**Rural/community pharmacy outcomes (broad chronic disease)**
- CDC, *Preventing Chronic Disease* 2020 — "Telepharmacy and Quality of Medication Use in Rural Areas, 2013–2019" (comparative study, rural telepharmacies vs. traditional pharmacies)

**HIV**
- Margusino-Framinan et al. 2019, *Telemedicine and e-Health* — 3-year cohort observational study of pharmacist teleconsultation with home antiretroviral delivery (adherence, viral load, CD4 count, cost, satisfaction)
- Atrium Health study, *PMC* 2023 — retrospective cohort study of clinic-embedded/telepharmacy-supported specialty pharmacy service on ART adherence, viral suppression, CD4 count

**Asthma**
- PARTE pilot study, *Telemed J E Health* 2012 — RCT of telephone pharmacist consultations vs. standard care in rural asthma patients
- Community Pharmacist Telephonic Medication Reviews with Uncontrolled Asthma Patients (pilot study, Missouri)
- Bynum et al. 2001 — telepharmacy counseling on inhaler technique in adolescents with asthma, rural Arkansas

**Heart failure**
- Impact of Pharmacist Telehealth Comanagement for Heart Failure, *JACC: Advances* 2025 — nonrandomized controlled study (quasi-randomized rollout) of telehealth pharmacist comanagement for HFrEF
- NPS MedicineWise app pilot RCT — single-blind RCT of app-supported, tiered pharmacist-led adherence intervention in heart failure
