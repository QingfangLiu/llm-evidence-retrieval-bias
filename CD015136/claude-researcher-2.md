**Cardiometabolic disease (diabetes, hypertension, hyperlipidemia)**

- **STIC2IT trial** (Lauffenburger et al., *JAMA Intern Med* 2019) — Cluster-RCT, 14 primary care sites, 4,078 adults with poor adherence/control on glucose-lowering, antihypertensive, or statin therapy. Remote pharmacist intervention (phone behavioral interviewing, texting, pillboxes, mailed reports) vs. usual care. The intervention improved medication adherence but did not significantly improve disease control on LDL, systolic BP, or HbA1c. Protocol paper: Lauffenburger et al., *Am Heart J* 2016 (PMID 27659887).

- **UAE pharmacist-led MTM RCT** (type 2 diabetes) — RCT of a multifactorial pharmacist-led intervention with face-to-face counselling, medication booklets, and a mobile app in 192 patients with type 2 diabetes, measuring medication adherence via refill ratio and questionnaire.

- **Rural clinic pharmacist telehealth pilot** — Non-randomized pilot/feasibility study of a pharmacist-led, remote, centralized telehealth service for chronic disease management, with A1c outcomes tracked pre/post enrollment.

- **Pharmacist-led telemedicine for hypertension (pilot)** — Single-center, prospective, 3-month RCT of a pharmacist-designed app ("Kidney Health") in 86 CKD patients, assessing medication adherence, kidney function, blood pressure, and glucose.

**Heart failure**

- **Pharmacist-led CHF adherence RCT** (China, 2021–2023) — Prospective, multicenter RCT across five hospitals comparing a pharmacist-led management model using a social media platform against usual care for medication adherence in chronic heart failure.

**Older adults / polypharmacy**

- **Pharmacist-led home televisit trial** — Two-site cluster RCT of pharmacist-led home televisits (medication reconciliation and actual-use assessment) versus usual care, targeting appropriate medication use, discrepancies, adherence, and adverse drug reactions in older adults.

**Cancer**

- **Breast cancer remote pharmacy follow-up RCT** (China, 2023–2025) — 178 non-metastatic breast cancer patients randomized to app-based remote pharmaceutical follow-up vs. routine care; severe adverse drug reactions occurred in 20.2% of the intervention group vs. 50.0% of controls (P<.01), with better adherence, knowledge, and quality-of-life scores.

**Anticoagulation (warfarin)**

- **Alfalfa App RCT** (post cardiac valve replacement, China) — 405 participants managed via an "Internet + pharmacy care" app vs. in-person pharmacist-led anticoagulation clinic visits; time-in-therapeutic-range was 66.46% vs. 46.65% (P<.001), with higher monitoring frequency in the app group.

- **Home warfarin telemedicine RCT** (criteria-driven, CoaguChek) — 87 patients randomized in a trial of telemedicine-supported patient self-management of warfarin using criteria-driven healthcare interactions over 10 months.

- **Internet-based warfarin management after mechanical valve replacement** — Large-scale, prospective, multicenter RCT testing whether internet-based warfarin management increases time-in-therapeutic-range and reduces anticoagulation complications compared with conventional face-to-face management.

- **Chiang Mai postal warfarin telepharmacy** (retrospective cohort) — Retrospective study of 149 outpatients in a postal warfarin delivery program with pharmacist telephone follow-up; therapeutic INR prevalence was 52.3% before vs. 45% after the program.

**HIV**

- **Teleconsultation + home antiretroviral delivery (Spain)** — Study of 854 teleconsultations paired with home antiretroviral delivery, reporting 100% treatment adherence and maintained viral suppression, alongside cost and time savings and high patient-reported satisfaction.

- **"Virtual Hospital" RCT** (HIV, Spain) — RCT with 42 patients followed through an internet-based homecare model (including telepharmacy) versus 41 in standard care; no significant differences in viral load, CD4 count, or adherence between groups.

**Infectious disease network (mixed inpatient/outpatient)**

- **TELnet@NRW stepped-wedge cluster RCT** — Multicenter, stepped-wedge cluster RCT (2017–2020) across Germany testing a telemedicine network of expert teleconsultations for ICU and outpatient infectious disease management, with adherence to Choosing Wisely recommendations as the primary outcome.
